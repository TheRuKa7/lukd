# ZERO-SUM: OMEN

Entry for the [Official Unity Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/official-unity-game-jam-2026). Theme: **Lucky Number**.

In a world where every soul's luck floats above its head as a number, a nameless wanderer with no number at all tells the city its lucky number, and becomes the most wanted person alive. An anime 2.5D party RPG that travels through lands inspired by Japanese, Chinese, Indian, Greek and American myth and folklore, where every number spins and you **tap to stop it**.
