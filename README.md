# ZERO-SUM: OMEN

Entry for the [Official Unity Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/official-unity-game-jam-2026). Theme: **Lucky Number**.

Every number in the city spins. Tap to stop them, and tell the game your lucky number first.

A cinematic 2.5D squad roguelite with one verb and no HUD. Animation, motion graphics and cinematics drive the game.

- **Design doc:** [`docs/GDD.md`](docs/GDD.md). Pillars, rules, story and cinematics, motion direction, Unity/WebGL plan, 7-day schedule.
- **Feel prototype:** [`prototype/index.html`](prototype/index.html). Open it in a browser. Tap/Space stops a number; tap a hero (or press 1/2/3) to choose who acts. It is the reference for the Unity beat sequences and rules.

Hard deadline: **Sat 14 Nov 2026, 12:00 UTC.** A WebGL build is required.
