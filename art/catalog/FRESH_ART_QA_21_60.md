## Scope and finding

All40 boards were inspected in the generated contact sheets `build/higgs_art_contact_21.jpg` and `build/higgs_art_contact_41.jpg`. Seven high-risk boards were then inspected individually; the viewer displayed full boards at1920px width from3504px sources. Fine labels/fingers cannot be certified from contact thumbnails. The supplied designs are broadly coherent, detailed matte ink/cel proposals with recognizable occupations and distinct silhouettes.

**One focused correction is required: board39, Aka.** Ao has exactly one fox tail. Aka’s side and unobstructed rear views have no fox tail, despite the red twin’s mirror identity. Recreate the red twin with exactly one visible tail, the same twin face/build and the ponytail placed on the opposite anatomical side to Ao. Preserve the red/cold-foxfire distinction. This is a character continuity issue, not a request to polish invisible tiny trim. The hash below identifies the reviewed original; any replacement needs a new targeted check.

No additional major regeneration is recommended in21–60 from this pass. Moirai has three sisters in every direction, with side-view occlusion rather than missing members; Ox-Head/Horse-Face likewise retains both wardens. Their source tools and red-thread/group silhouettes are coherent, and they are presented as composed guardians rather than gore or defeated monsters. Debt Hound57 remains visibly mechanical, with a muzzle, load-bearing legs, reserve vessel and overhead wheel anchor. Auditor50’s licensed device remains on the anatomical right wrist across front/profile/back.

## Nonblocking production notes

- Hap29 preserves the star robe, two dice eyes and digit orbit. The front cupped die is not clearly carried into the profile hand poses; resolve that close-up prop contact before using this sheet as an animation construction master. Exact orbit lettering remains a separate authored layer; the concept does not certify its typography.
- Little Lantern40 carries the lantern by a handle rather than clearly on a stick. Keep the approved stick/handle construction explicit in the eventual prop model.
- Generated numerals or tiny marks on some props/clothes are illustration details, not trusted mechanic/identity text. Production exact labels remain code/vector text.
- The cultural keeper figures are respectful visual proposals under this brief. No traditional garment/iconographic authenticity or community approval is asserted. Chitragupta’s palm-leaf/stylus construction and other sacred details still require the already specified source check before regional public content.
- Generic profile-only prop occlusion is not counted as a missing limb/body. Source four-camera Blender sets remain the spatial authority until drawn turnaround construction is accepted.
