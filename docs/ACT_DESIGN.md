One section per land: its story in one paragraph, the Land Law (its superstition as a rule), the act map, encounters, the keeper trial, the boss (full sheet in [`BESTIARY.md`](BESTIARY.md)), side quests, Hap's Whispers, and what the act teaches. ## 0. How every act is built

- **Map:** approximately 8–10 node groups, with branches converging on a boss. Campaign segment budgets are 35/45/50/50/45/50/40 minutes (315 total) plus 45–105 optional minutes; 6–7 hours is a hypothesis, not observed completion. Trial groups and dungeons may contain several short beats; count them in the segment budget rather than hiding extra acts.
- **Node types:** Small Audit · Big Audit · Elite · Keeper's Trial · Market · Shrine/Forge · Teahouse/Campfire · Story · **Open Hand shrine** (Hap: re-deal one offer, or pray for Favour) · **Hap's Whisper** (a coincidence event) · Boss.
- **Pacing rule:** never two fights in a row without a choice; never more than two non-fight nodes in a row. A story node never interrupts a Market.
- **Every act spotlights one new decision layer:** a Land Law/verb and its narrative consequence. It may introduce more than one companion (River Empire) or several supporting items, but teaches them separately; it does not guarantee one-and-only-one character/system. Final camp reconciles missed core stages before ending commitment.
- **Land Law:** the land's superstition as a global rule for every fight there, shown on the reel frame as a small icon. Counter-play always exists inside that land (a companion, a charm or a verb).

**Regional-rules backlog gate F-LAND:** before enabling a Land Law, author its stable ID, scope/entry/exit, exact event/input domain, numeric modifier/order, stacking/reset, visible telegraph and same-land accessible counter in typed content. Test each chosen digit, every required party, replacement/terminal overlaps and save/retry. The regional briefs below do not substitute for those records. Keep numeric effects disabled until the SYSTEMS/content gate passes; no missing values are inferred from folklore.

## 1. Prologue: Meridian Undercity

### 1A. Required jam route:10–15 minutes

Rain Alley rescue (jar−6/Ama+6) → Naming choice/detection → **one Debt Hound, NIL solo** → Vesper joins → **one Debt Hound, both heroes** → **choose one free charm from Horseshoe/Black Cat/Ofuda** → **Varro alone** → authored mercy/Seal 1 → rooftop safe-family/map scene → results/retry. Three encounters exactly. One Meridian stage supplies lighting/prop variants; no Maeve/Hina, Collector add encounter, full map, charged ultimate, crafting or Link Art. JAM_SCRIPT/FIRST_10_MINUTES specify lines, tutorial exceptions and targets. Varro has expiry recovery even without a successful defence (BESTIARY).

### 1B. Full-campaign Prologue:35 minutes

**Story.** Meridian's Remainder Wards are where low Omens end up. NIL appears with a NaN, tells the city a lucky number and becomes the most wanted person alive. Vesper takes the job of keeping NIL alive; Varro takes the job of collecting.

**Land Law: Debt.** Every third round the Collectors charge interest: the party loses 5% of its Balance unless the round scored a pattern. (Teaches: patterns are protection, not just damage.)

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | Rain Alley 9; the Naming shrine; lucky-number choice |
| 2 | Small Audit (tutorial) | One Debt Hound; NIL alone; explicit legal first-round Jackpot example |
| 3 | Story + Small Audit | Vesper joins; one Debt Hound; choose legal two-hero order |
| 4a / 4b | Reward / Forge | Gus's first charm **or** campaign-only forge defence to recruit Maeve early; either route also grants the first charm choice |
| 5 | Big Audit | Collector squad (armoured; parry practice) |
| 6 | Boss | **Varro Kade, the Ledgerhound** at the drain gates |

**Enemies.** Debt Hound (fast wheel, weighted to 4), Collector (armoured: Iron faces), Noiseling (its wheel scrambles once per spin).
**Reel object:** a dead three-tube Nixie sign. **Music:** synth noir, rain, Nixie hum.
**Side quests:** *Livia's window* (starts the Varro arc) · *Gus's ledger* (pay off the charm "credit" for a discount later).
**Hap's Whispers:** a coin lands on its edge (free re-spin); a black cat crosses the alley (choose: Bust → Pair once, or nothing); "Someone shares your birthday" (+5 fen).
**Teaches:** stop, reel, Pair, Jackpot, Parry, choosing strikers, Cascade, Zero-Sum, ultimates.

Ultimates, economy/Whispers and the extra Collector fight in 1B are full-campaign progression, not additions to the jam by implication. Maeve's missed recruitment recovers at Hyakki's caravan Teahouse or final camp. Rooftop confession/receipt sets the Hyakki toll-ledger lead. Personal rest: rooftop safe family; peak: detection and Varro's mercy. Required journal/Seal outcomes apply identically after skip.

## 2. Act I: Hyakki (Japanese folklore)

**Story.** Yokai fade when nobody believes in them. Tamamo promised to deliver the still-free Remainder in exchange for yokai permanence; she has not already captured/sold NIL. Hina wants to remain able to change, even before she turns 100. NIL's choice answers her fear without aging her.

**Land Law: Shi and Ku.** A 4 on the reel curses its striker (−10% damage next strike); a 9 curses the party's next parry. Hina in the party makes 9 s safe and turns them into +9 Mult. Sevens and eights are blessed: +1 Mult each.

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | Tetsu's ferry; the Moon Pier |
| 2 | Small Audit | Chōchin-obake (lantern ghosts) on the pier deck |
| 3 | Story | The Night Parade; Hina joins (party of 3, bonds, Link Arts) |
| 4a / 4b | Shrine / Hap's Whisper | Ofuku's Seven Lucky Gods shrine (free charm, charm sets) **or** a fox wedding in the rain (Whisper) |
| 5 | Big Audit | Kasa-obake pack (hopping wheels) |
| 6 | Teahouse | Party swap; Vesper/Hina spotlight; Maeve caravan catch-up if missed |
| 7a / 7b | Elite / Market | Kitsune twins Ao and Aka **or** the kappa river market (Kyūsuke quest) |
| 8 | Keeper's Trial | **Enma's gate**: judged, not fought in anger. Rule: you may not parry; you must win within 4 rounds. NIL learns **Carry** |
| 9 | Boss | **Tamamo, the Nine-Tailed Auditor** |

**Enemies.** Chōchin-obake (lantern; its wheel lights only odd faces), Kasa-obake (one-eyed umbrella; its wheel hops back a face once per spin), Kappa (lose strength if they bow: land a 5 to make them bow), Oni enforcer (heavy; immune to Pairs).
**Reel object:** a torii with three hanging lanterns. **Music:** shamisen, shakuhachi, taiko over synth pads.
**Side quests:** *Cucumbers for Kyūsuke* (secret recruit 5) · *One eye a day* (help Ofuku paint 7 daruma eyes; reward: the Daruma charm upgrade).
**Hap's Whispers:** a fox wedding (rain in sunshine: free Shrine visit); a tea stalk floating upright (*chabashira*: +1 charm slot this act); a crow at a crossroads (choose a curse to accept for a rare charm).
**Teaches:** charms and sets, curses, Reaper's Toll, party of 3, bonds and Link Arts.

Rest: Teahouse; peak: Tamamo's offer and Hina claiming personhood at 99. Release shifts the toll obligation to the Luck Exchange, creating Act II's lead. Ofuku's message and Tamamo aftermath advance Hina's stages; `piece.enma_page` is a protected record. Enma's required campaign trial supplies Carry; no future boss assumes an optional jam trial was completed.

## 3. Act II: the River Empire (Chinese folk religion and festival culture)

**Story.** The Luck Exchange trades Omens; Jin Chan's fortune compounds while debt compounds for everyone else. Below the river, the Ten Courts' count of the dead has always been short, because the Basilica rounds the dead too. Ba Jinling burns her father's books; Bai Si learns his count was wrong.

**Land Law: Interest.** Mult carries over: each round's final Mult adds 10% of itself to the next round's (resets on a Bust). Enemies gain the same on their side. (Teaches: streaks.)

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | Lantern Festival; Little Lantern; Jinling's pawnshop |
| 2 | Small Audit | Abacus Golems in the festival crowd |
| 3 | Market | **Wheel crafting** and **oracle sticks** unlock (Luck Exchange back alleys; temple of sticks) |
| 4a / 4b | Big Audit / Hap's Whisper | Debt Junk on the river **or** Granny Liu Shun's mahjong table (secret recruit 6) |
| 5 | Story | The river's end; falling into the Ten Courts (art style switches to ink-wash) |
| 6 | Keeper's Trial | **Ox-Head and Horse-Face**: both must be bested in the same round |
| 7 | Elite | Hungry Ghost swarm (they eat fen on every Bust) |
| 8 | Keeper's Trial | **Judge Cui**: supplied trial stake/Brush shows costs safely (BESTIARY); grants Rewrite; Bai Si joins |
| 9 | Teahouse | Party swap; Jinling and Bai Si's first scene ("You buy debts?" "I burn them.") |
| 10 | Boss | **Chancellor Jin Chan, the Golden Toad** |

**Enemies.** Abacus Golem (its wheel shows only 0–5), Debt Junk (fires interest: its damage grows every round), Golden Koi (split into two when hit by a Pair), Hungry Ghost (eats fen), Jiangshi (hops; can't be hit on even rounds).
**Reel object:** a golden abacus (harbor); a floating ghost ledger (underworld). **Music:** erhu and guzheng over synth; the underworld is silent except brush strokes.
**Side quests:** *Little Lantern's family* · *Thirteen Orphans* (Liu Shun's mahjong ladder) · *Burn the books* (help Jinling find every debt ledger).
**Hap's Whispers:** a red envelope on the street (+8 fen, or give it to a child for Karma later); a sticks cylinder that won't stop shaking (a free oracle stick); the Lantern Festival riddle (answer: a digit).
**Teaches:** fen economy, markets, wheel crafting, oracle sticks, Ink, interest/streaks.

Rest: quiet Courts/Teahouse; peak: Jinling burns enforcement copies after protecting the true book. Ghost/living records expose the same fractions and direct Act III. Jinling guest→permanent and BaiSi recruitment are distinct flags. A signed-but-withheld Livia order, seeded by Ivo's archive corrections, supplies a recoverable Act V lead. Liu Shun's optional recruit accepts a tile lesson or purse recovery; no required victory over her or unexplained mahjong mastery.

## 4. Act III: Śūnyapura (Indian mythology, astronomy and mathematics)

**Story.** The observatory first measured the Deficit. Surveyors suppress its numbers with two invented brass **Eclipse Engines “Rahu” and “Ketu”**, visibly labelled machines, not the revered celestial figures. Shunya proves NIL's unlisted account while learning to ask what NIL wants done with that knowledge.

**Land Law: Karma.** Parries and Zero-Sums bank Karma; declared curses may debit it; spend on rerolls/lethal protection (SYSTEMS). On act exit persistent Karma becomes floor(0.75×Karma), not an undeclared full carry. Chitragupta uses a separate trial counter so a full persistent bank cannot make the trial impossible.

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | Monsoon arrival; the observatory; Shunya joins (**Karma** and **Navaratna gems** unlock) |
| 2 | Hap's Whisper | Baital's riddle at the banyan (answer a digit; reward: a gem) |
| 3 | Small Audit | Brass Yantra Guardians (animated instruments) |
| 4a / 4b | Market / Shrine | Gem bazaar **or** an Open Hand shrine in a temple courtyard |
| 5 | Big Audit (dungeon) | The Stepwell of a Thousand Steps with Meera: 3 short fights, descending; Monsoon Nāgas |
| 6 | Keeper's Trial | **Chitragupta's Hall**: every action is shown on palm leaves; the trial is won by finishing with more Karma than you started. NIL learns **Place Value**; **the Remainder reveal** |
| 7 | Teahouse | The quietest scene in the game; party swap |
| 8 | Elite | Basilica surveyors with a Noise storm (wheels scramble every 2 rounds) |
| 9 | Boss | **Eclipse Engines “Rahu” and “Ketu”**: linked machine stakes, telegraphed equalization/occlusion |

**Enemies.** Vetāla (asks a riddle; answer correctly and it skips its strike), Brass Yantra Guardian (its wheel turns like a sundial: one face per round, predictable), Monsoon Nāga (floods a reel tube: that digit counts double for both sides).
**Reel object:** three observatory sundial dials. **Music:** sitar and tabla over synth; temple bells; rain on stone.
**Side quests:** *Meera's brother* (karma record; payoff in the Hall) · *The star catalogue* (help Vidya Rao finish it) · *Lemon and seven chillies* (replace them on every door the storm tore down).
**Hap's Whispers:** a peacock dancing before the rain (next fight starts with Favour warm); a lizard's chirp at the right moment (choose: re-roll a gem); a monsoon break (a free Teahouse).
**Teaches:** Karma, gems (pattern levels), the Ledger Tree, two-target balancing.
**Cultural/source care:** celestial Rahu/Ketu are never enemies; the Basilica's engines have bolts, cables and operator stamps, no sacred body/head imagery. Source research is not a consultant sign-off. Sensitive unverified material is removed/fictionalized, with actual review evidence recorded if available.

Rest: silent observatory steps after proof; peak: Remainder reveal and reopening the sky. Obtain `piece.remainder_equation`; co-author consent is pre-finale, publication epilogue-only. Differing regional possibilities seed Positive-Sum. Thale's reaction accelerates the Sieve, causing the next act's urgency.

## 5. Act IV: Kroton Nova (Greek mythology and Pythagorean number mysticism)

**Story.** A white island city built as a sieve. The Synod drops citizens with composite Omens into the undercity and calls it purification. Prima, "the even prime", defects to save a school; the Moirai test whether NIL's thread deserves to be cut.

**Land Law: Indivisibility.** Prime strike values pierce armour; composite strike values deal half damage. The Sieve shakes every 3 rounds: one composite face on each wheel falls off for the rest of the fight. (Teaches: wheel crafting under pressure; number properties.)

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | Noon arrival; Niko falls through the Sieve; Prima escorts the party (**Awakenings** explained) |
| 2 | Small Audit | Bronze Automata (Talos-like; immune until a prime lands) |
| 3 | Market | The Agora of Primes (gems and wheel crafting at a prime discount) |
| 4 | Story | The undercity of the fallen; Mother Kallisto |
| 5a / 5b | Big Audit / Hap's Whisper | Composite Knights at the Sieve gate **or** a Siren who sings numbers (answer by landing her sequence) |
| 6 | Story + Big Audit | Prima defects; Harpies over the drop |
| 7 | Keeper's Trial ×3 | **Moirai**: Clotho legal rising sequence; Lachesis temporary trial links; Atropos expendable charm or supplied ribbon, never protected proof. NIL learns Factor; Awakenings unlock without requiring completed optional bonds |
| 8 | Teahouse | Party swap; Prima and Shunya ("Is zero prime?" "Zero is *busy*.") |
| 9 | Boss | **Archon Seventeen** |

**Enemies.** Bronze Automaton (immune until a prime strike lands), Harpy (steals a reel tube for a round), Composite Knight (shield that only composite strikes can break: the Law turned around), Siren (sings a digit sequence; land it to silence her).
**Reel object:** three marble columns with mosaic numerals. **Music:** lyre and bouzouki over synth; waves; chanting of primes.
**Side quests:** *Niko's goats* (always one short; the last goat is in the Moirai's cave) · *Kallisto's ladder* (raise the undercity) · *Spit three times* (a superstition mini-game: dodge the evil eye).
**Hap's Whispers:** an owl at noon (+1 Mult per prime this act); a broken mirror (accept 7 rounds of bad luck for a legendary charm); a thread caught on your sleeve (Lachesis's gift: see the next boss rule early).
**Teaches:** Awakenings, number properties (primes, perfect numbers, golden ratio), wheel crafting mastery.

Rest: undercity/recovery; peak: Prima's school rescue and later rescue of her teacher. The Archon falls because his damaged machine gives way, not because Civic 17 somehow became composite when its Audit readout fell. First gear/finance archive gives Maeve a protected forging material and leads to the House. Hina's Awakening changes acceptance/kit, never age; she remains 99.

## 6. Act V: the Crossroads Territory (American folklore)

**Story.** In Lucky Strike the night loops. The House financed the Basilica in exchange for a night that never ends, so that his granddaughter Fibi never reaches the dawn. Vesper faces the hand she rigged here; Varro learns Thale lied about his sister.

**Land Law: the House.** Every fight is a wager: before it starts you may bet fen or Balance for a bigger reward. The night **loops**: every 3 rounds the fight rewinds (enemy Balance and your reel reset), but your charms, Karma and Mult carry over. (Teaches: building inside a loop; Deals.)

| Node | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keeper's Trial | **The midnight crossroads**: the Stranger offers a deal. The trial is honest: win a hand of the Stranger's game. NIL learns **Deal**; **Crossroads Deals** and **Wagers** unlock |
| 2 | Small Audit | Card-Sharp Automata on Route 7 |
| 3 | Story | Lucky Strike iteration 1,597 after 1,596 completed repeats; Fibi joins and stage 1 investigates familiar routines |
| 4a / 4b | Market / Hap's Whisper | Gus Mendoza's second shop **or** Penny on the railway (Ghost Train quest) |
| 5 | Big Audit | Tumbleweed Golems in a card dust storm |
| 6 | Story | Varro returns; withheld-release proof and courier route can save Livia even if her window was missed; guest unlock afterward; Sheriff Vance and motel investigation give Fibi stage 2 |
| 7 | Elite | **The Headless Horseman** of the hollow (his wheel has no top half: 5–9 missing) |
| 8 | Elite | **The Ghost Train**: its cars are Loop Echoes of your own party |
| 9 | Story | Vesper's honest hand; recovered fatal-maintenance clause; Fibi stage 3 consents to cancellation |
| 10 | Boss | **The House, "Mr. Eighty-Nine"** |
| 11 | Story / rest | Contract cancellation and dawn; Fibi stage 4 and `piece.black_book_page`; Vesper's marked deck protected |

**Enemies.** Card-Sharp Automaton (deals itself a hidden face), Tumbleweed Golem (rolls: its wheel spins backwards), Loop Echo (copies your last strike), the Headless Horseman, the Ghost Train.
**Reel object:** a giant slot machine. **Music:** slide guitar, harmonica, a slot-machine choir over synth.
**Side quests:** *Penny's 1,597th penny* · *The dead man's hand* (a poker side game) · *Livia's exemption* (Varro's arc payoff).
**Hap's Whispers:** a four-leaf clover in the cracked asphalt (1-in-4 duplicates for one fight); a shooting star over the desert (wish: re-deal every offer in the next Market); a penny heads-up (+1 fen per 1 landed this act).
**Teaches:** Deals, Wagers, loops.
**Cultural care:** no Indigenous sacred traditions; draw on tall tales, the blues crossroads legend, immigrant charms and Sleepy Hollow.

Rest: motel at 3a.m.; peak: first new dawn. Outside time advances only the anchored night; NIL anchors new party memories, so Fibi cannot predict a prior meeting with NIL that never happened. Story resets have stable reward IDs; no fen/proof duplication. Six Seal alarms cause guard recall, not automatic ritual-gate opening.

**Land Law: Fixed Fate.** SYSTEMS/BESTIARY specify phase-limited slowing/face removal, minimum legal options and the final guided ritual. This pressure cannot remove a required callback/ending choice or strand the player with an empty wheel. Phase 2 changes to a visible arrangement puzzle; Phase 3 recalls are scripted from roster rather than unlocked by absent faces.

Rest: final camp; peak: exposed cage, collective recalls and chosen Open Hand. STORY §9 owns exact predicates and idempotent flags. No precondition depends on a post-ending birthday/publication, a secret recruit or active-party equipment.
