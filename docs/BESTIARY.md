# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Bestiary: Enemies, Elites, Keepers and Bosses

Every opponent's wheel, behaviour, telegraph and counter. Balances scale per act ([`TUNING.md`](TUNING.md) §3). Stories in [`STORY.md`](STORY.md); where they appear in [`ACT_DESIGN.md`](ACT_DESIGN.md).

**Design rules for every opponent**
1. **One idea per enemy**, readable from its wheel alone within one spin.
2. **Telegraph before punish.** Any special move is shown one round ahead (an icon on its wheel and a sound).
3. **A counter exists in the same land** (a hero, a charm, a verb or a Land Law interaction).
4. **Defeat is "Settled", never gore.** Enemies round down into digits and blow away.
5. **Keepers are never enemies.** Their fights are trials with rules, and they end satisfied, not beaten.

## 4. Bosses (Auditors)

Each boss has a **rule** that changes how you play, **phases**, **telegraphs**, a **cinematic ultimate**, and an aftermath that changes the world.

### Varro Kade, the Ledgerhound · Prologue · Omen 61 · Balance 1,200
- **Rule, the Debt Jar:** each round he steals your **last reel digit** into a glass jar on his belt (that tube goes dark next round). Three parries shatter all jars and return the digits as free strikes.
- **Phase 1 (100–50%):** two Debt Hounds assist. **Phase 2 (<50%):** "Final Notice": his strikes double, but a jar shatters on every Pair.
- **Telegraph:** he taps a jar before stealing; the jar's tube flashes red.
- **Ultimate, *Foreclosure*:** at 25% he slams every jar into the reel: if you parry the next strike, they all return to you as a free Jackpot.
- **Teaches:** defence matters; the reel is a resource.
- **Aftermath:** spared; returns in Act V.

### Tamamo, the Nine-Tailed Auditor · Act I · Omen 99 (per tail) · Balance 6,000
- **Rule, Nine Tails:** nine tails, each with its own small Balance; she can't be hit until three tails are down. **Foxfire illusion faces** appear on your wheels: they show one digit and land as another (a faint flicker gives them away).
- **Shi and Ku:** 9s curse you unless Hina is in the party (then 9s burn a tail outright).
- **Phases:** 9 tails → 6 → 3, each phase adds one illusion face per wheel. At 3 tails she becomes a giant fox (camera pulls back; the pier shakes).
- **Telegraph:** foxfire gathers on the tail about to strike.
- **Ultimate, *Hundred Demons' Night*:** the Night Parade crosses the screen; every wheel shows only illusions for one round. Counter: Hina's *Hundred-Year Night* cancels it (the best Link moment in the jam).
- **Teaches:** read carefully; synergy (Hina).
- **Aftermath:** asks what a yokai is worth without belief; retreats into legend.

### Chancellor Jin Chan, the Golden Toad · Act II · Omen 88 · Balance 35,000
- **Rule, Interest:** his Balance gains 8% each round. **Jackpot tax:** he takes 20% of every Jackpot's damage as coins that heal him.
- **Phase 2, the Swallow:** he swallows a hero (removed from the party); land a Pair to make him cough them up, with a free strike.
- **Telegraph:** coins rain before he swallows; the target hero glows gold.
- **Ultimate, *Golden Compound*:** his interest triples for 3 rounds unless you Zero-Sum (the ledger balances, the interest is cancelled).
- **Teaches:** burst damage; streaks; the economy.
- **Aftermath:** bankrupted by his daughter; laughs until he cries.

### Rahu & Ketu, the Eclipse · Act III · Omen: eclipsed · Balance 2 × 100,000
- **Rule, two bodies, one fight:** Rahu (the crowned head) swallows one reel tube each round (it goes dark). Ketu (the serpent body with a comet tail) strikes with a blind random wheel you can't read. If their Balances drift **more than 10% apart**, they rejoin and both heal 20%.
- **Zero-Sum hits both equally**, which makes it the key pattern of the fight.
- **Phases:** partial eclipse → totality (the screen goes dark except the reel and their outlines) → the diamond ring (one round of blinding light: every wheel slows).
- **Telegraph:** the sun's shadow edge shows which tube Rahu will swallow.
- **Ultimate, *Grahan*:** totality for 3 rounds; Karma decides who can see their wheels.
- **Teaches:** balance damage between two targets.
- **Presentation:** cosmic, tragic and beautiful, never grotesque; reviewed by consultants.

### Archon Seventeen · Act IV · Omen 17 · Balance 1,500,000
- **Rule, the Sieve:** each round he strikes digits off **your** wheels in the order of the Sieve of Eratosthenes (multiples of 2, then 3, then 5…). Composite strike values are halved (the Land Law).
- **Phase 2, Purification:** he removes his own composite faces and becomes faster.
- **Telegraph:** the next multiple glows on every wheel.
- **Ultimate, *Seventeen Years*:** a cicada-swarm cinematic; for one round only primes can be stopped.
- **Teaches:** wheel crafting; Prima's primes; Factor (split a composite into primes).
- **Aftermath:** falls through his own sieve; Prima catches him.

### The House, "Mr. Eighty-Nine" (Octavian Nine) · Act V · Omen 89 · Balance 12,000,000
- **Rule, the Loop:** every 3 rounds time rewinds: his Balance and your reel reset, but your charms, Karma and Mult persist. He **copies your last pattern** against you. Mid-fight he offers a **deal** (accept: a huge boon with a price that lasts the rest of the game).
- **Phase 2:** the loop shortens to 2 rounds. **Phase 3:** dawn approaches; the loop breaks if you win one loop without a Bust.
- **Telegraph:** his backwards pocket watch ticks; the numbers on it show the rounds left in the loop.
- **Ultimate, *House Always Wins*:** a slot machine the size of the casino; three reels he controls. Counter: Fibi's *Spiral Deal* (a 5-tube reel) outscores it.
- **Teaches:** build inside a loop; read the deal.
- **Aftermath:** lets the dawn come; says goodbye to Fibi.

### Comptroller Lucan Thale · Finale · Omen 50 (steel) · Balance 99,999,999
- **Phase 1, Regression to the Mean:** every wheel compresses toward 5 (faces far from 5 shrink each round). Counter: Cascades through 4-5-6.
- **Phase 2, Fixed Fate:** wheels stop spinning. A puzzle round: you **arrange** your heroes' digits instead of stopping them, with Thale removing one option per turn.
- **Phase 3, Zero:** NIL alone. Each companion returns as a Link Art when NIL lands that companion's digit, with a line from their bond arc. Win condition: the last strike's digit is NIL's own.
- **Telegraph:** the Basilica's clock hands point at the face Thale will freeze.
- **Ultimate, *The Final Audit*:** the whole nave becomes a ledger; every number on screen is counted out loud.
- **Aftermath:** depends on the ending; in the true ending he tends the pigeons at the Open Hand shrine.

## 5. Post-game superboss

### The Default · the Endless Ledger only
The world without luck: a still, perfectly grey figure. It has **no wheel**. Its "strikes" are the absence of yours: each round it removes one face from every wheel in the game. You win by landing an Omen Overwrite before your wheels run out. Hap's Favour is at its strongest here: the god is fighting alongside you, quietly.
