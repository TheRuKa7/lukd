# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Bestiary: Enemies, Elites, Keepers and Bosses

Every opponent's wheel, behaviour, telegraph and counter. Balances scale per act ([`TUNING.md`](TUNING.md) §3). Stories in [`STORY.md`](STORY.md); where they appear in [`ACT_DESIGN.md`](ACT_DESIGN.md).

Updated 7 November 2026 under [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). **Specified, not implemented or playtested.** Jam opponents: one solo Debt Hound, one two-hero Debt Hound, then Varro alone. All other enemies/trials/bosses are expansion content. SYSTEMS owns resolution/ordering and PROJECT_STATUS records evidence. Named Civic values below are identity badges outside combat; normalized Audit Omens begin at 99 and fall with staked Balance, except a clearly labelled noncombat trial objective.

**Design rules for every opponent**
1. **One idea per enemy**, readable from its wheel alone within one spin.
2. **Telegraph before punish.** Any special move is shown one round ahead (an icon on its wheel and a sound).
3. **A counter exists in the same land** (a hero, a charm, a verb or a Land Law interaction).
4. **Audit settlement is not civic death.** Balance 0 ends combat. Named people kneel/withdraw alive for their aftermath; spirits disperse without being killed; appropriate constructs/Noise may dissolve or fall inert. Never apply one disintegration prefab to all actors.
5. **Keepers are never enemies.** Their fights are trials with rules, and they end satisfied, not beaten.

Presentation: solemn music, no damage/settlement animation on a keeper; trial objectives are lanterns, scrolls or threads, clearly labelled as progress rather than Civic Omen. Keepers bow when satisfied. These trials unlock required verbs on the campaign main path; failure offers a retry at the trial checkpoint and readable help, not a dead keeper or lost critical item.

Trial safety contracts: Enma's four rounds use a provided nonlethal objective and no damaging enemy action while Parry is disabled; repeated failure offers slower wheels without changing lore. Ox-Head/Horse-Face test two objective seals, not their bodies, and allow target preview/retry. Judge Cui's trial-only Brush costs a displayed 10 trial Balance per rewrite; supplied trial stake 100 and replenished attempt prevent resource lock; unused trial stake is not currency. Chitragupta starts a separate trial Karma counter at 0, supplies safe 0-defence/Zero-Sum opportunities and succeeds at counter≥1; it does not require exceeding a full persistent Karma cap. Lachesis temporarily links the trial party so no optional bond is required. Atropos permits cutting a supplied ribbon if no expendable owned charm is chosen; protected proof pieces can never be selected. The Stranger provides a fixed hand with rules and a retry, not a requirement to understand an external card game.

## 4. Bosses (Auditors)

Each boss has a **rule** that changes how you play, **phases**, **telegraphs**, a **cinematic ultimate**, and an aftermath that changes the world.

### Varro Kade, the Ledgerhound · Prologue · Civic 61 · Balance 1,200
- **Jam cast:** Varro alone, no Debt Hound adds or Foreclosure mechanic. His enemy wheel includes legal 0 for every chosen resonance. Audit Omen is normalized 99→00; Civic 61 remains a separately labelled identity badge.
- **Capture once:** announce before the first completed nonterminal player round. After that round scores, snapshot its **resolved slot 3** into one visible `debtToken`. Do not steal damage already settled or leave a two-slot reel.
- **Held state:** next three affected player rounds replace scoring slot 3 with 0 before Favour/Black Cat repair; keep the actual stopped digit for landing effects. Show held digit, affected-round count and successful-defence count.
- **Recovery:** after three successfully defended enemy actions (Parry/Fortune/Ofuda, each action counted once), open early. Otherwise expire after three affected player rounds, including Cascade rounds. No successful Parry is necessary to finish. Opening queues restoration, not retroactive rescoring or free strikes.
- **Restoration:** on the following player round, replace scoring slot 3 with the saved digit after its third stop, visibly; clear the token. No stacking/recapture. If combat ends first, clear transient debt with no extra event/reward.
- **Phase 2 (≤50%):** “Final Notice” announces the escalation. First enemy action after threshold crossing still uses base 40; following actions use 80, each previewed before the relevant player round. Do not double an already-announced hit retroactively.
- **Teaching line:** “Three defences—or three rounds. Then you get it back.” Exact rounding/event order and charm overlap: SYSTEMS. Any future Foreclosure/add variant needs a separate encounter ID and content gate; it is not implicitly enabled by an old prompt.
- **Teaches:** defence matters; the reel is a resource.
- **Aftermath:** spared; returns in Act V.

### Tamamo, the Nine-Tailed Auditor · Act I · Civic 99 · total Balance 6,000
- **Rule, Nine Tails:** nine tail objectives of 600 Balance each plus a 600-body stake total 6,000. The body unlocks only when all nine tails are exhausted; no double-paying damage to tail and body. A target preview selects a surviving tail; overflow does not secretly spill to the body.
- **Shi and Ku:** the Land Law applies. Active Hina makes 9s safe and each actual 9 deals an additional 90 to the selected tail through one declared nonrecursive event, rather than silently destroying a full tail regardless of Balance. The same-land Dispel option below remains available without her.
- **Phases:** 9 tails → 6 → 3, each phase adds one illusion face per wheel. At 3 tails she becomes a giant fox (camera pulls back; the pier shakes).
- **Telegraph:** foxfire gathers on the tail about to strike.
- **Ultimate, *Hundred Demons' Night*:** one telegraphed round after the six-tail threshold, parade imagery surrounds readable wheels. Hina's full-game *Hundred-Year Night* or the supplied Dispel action cancels the next illusion modifier. This is campaign spectacle, not a jam Link Art or age change.
- **Teaches:** read carefully; synergy (Hina).
- **Aftermath:** asks what a yokai is worth without belief; retreats into legend.

Tail thresholds 9/6/3 select three costume/effect phases; at zero tails expose the 600 body. A Foxfire face visibly names both stopped and resolved digit before commitment with a shape/flicker legend; actual face still drives landing effects, resolved face scoring. A supplied **Dispel** charge at encounter start and each phase transition (maximum one stored) cancels the next illusion modifier when selected before a player round, without consuming a scored strike or triggering charms. Readable illusion legends always permit play after charges run out. This avoids a required-counter dependency on active Hina or an optional charm. Her response: “Ninety-nine years. Enough nights to know you.” She never literally turns 100. All phases/fixtures remain expansion production work; these are specified values, not a verified boss.

### Chancellor Jin Chan, the Golden Toad · Act II · Omen 88 · Balance 35,000
- **Rule, Interest:** his Balance gains 8% each round. **Jackpot tax:** he takes 20% of every Jackpot's damage as coins that heal him.
- **Phase 2, the Swallow:** he swallows a hero (removed from the party); land a Pair to make him cough them up, with a free strike.
- **Telegraph:** coins rain before he swallows; the target hero glows gold.
- **Ultimate, *Golden Compound*:** his interest triples for 3 rounds unless you Zero-Sum (the ledger balances, the interest is cancelled).
- **Teaches:** burst damage; streaks; the economy.
- **Aftermath:** bankrupted by his daughter; laughs until he cries.

### Eclipse Engines “Rahu” and “Ketu” · Act III · two machine stakes of 100,000
- **Identity:** invented Basilica occluder disc and articulated surveyor rail, visibly brass/bolted and stamped as machinery. The revered celestial Rahu/Ketu are not targets, bodies, avatars or villains. Shutdown frees the observatory's view; it does not defeat a deity.
- **Linked stakes:** each starts 100,000. Compare remaining fractions, not absolute values: a gap greater than 10 percentage points queues a telegraphed equalization after the next player round; then heal each by floor(20% of its own maximum), capped at maximum, unless already terminal. Zero-Sum distributes its ordinary round damage equally, with odd remainder to the lower-index engine. Terminal state resolves before queued healing.
- **Occlusion:** the disc previews a tube by index one round ahead. For that tube on the next player round, suppress its face-enhancement bonus only; the accepted/resolved digit, base strike, pattern eligibility and essential UI remain readable. It never removes a slot or requires buying sight with Karma.
- **Rail attack:** random starting position is permissible, a hidden accepted digit is not. Show legal faces, stop cue and intent through visual/shaped/audio channels. Karma may reveal a future face as a bonus; it cannot purchase basic readability.
- **Phases:** partial occlusion → restrained totality around readable UI → diamond-ring colour key with reduced-motion/no-flash alternative. “Grahan” is the machinery's three-round suppression protocol, not an attack by a sacred being.
- **Teaches:** allocate damage and anticipate equalization; predictable counterplay beats blind guessing. Cultural/source review remains unverified; wording never claims consultants approved this encounter.

### Archon Seventeen · Act IV · Omen 17 · Balance 1,500,000
- **Rule, the Sieve:** each round he strikes digits off **your** wheels in the order of the Sieve of Eratosthenes (multiples of 2, then 3, then 5…). Composite strike values are halved (the Land Law).
- **Phase 2, Purification:** he removes his own composite faces and becomes faster.
- **Telegraph:** the next multiple glows on every wheel.
- **Ultimate, *Seventeen Years*:** a cicada-swarm cinematic; for one round only primes can be stopped.
- **Teaches:** wheel crafting; Prima's primes; Factor (split a composite into primes).
- **Aftermath:** falls through his own sieve; Prima catches him.

### The House, "Mr. Eighty-Nine" (Octavian Nine) · Act V · Omen 89 · Balance 12,000,000
- **Rule, the Loop:** every 3 rounds time rewinds: his Balance and your reel reset, but your charms, Karma and Mult persist. He **copies your last pattern** against you. Mid-fight he offers a **deal** (accept: a huge boon with a price that lasts the rest of the game).
- **Phase 2:** the loop shortens to 2 rounds. **Phase 3:** dawn approaches; the loop breaks if you win one loop without a Bust.
- **Telegraph:** his backwards pocket watch ticks; the numbers on it show the rounds left in the loop.
- **Ultimate, *House Always Wins*:** a slot machine the size of the casino; three reels he controls. Counter: Fibi's *Spiral Deal* (a 5-tube reel) outscores it.
- **Teaches:** build inside a loop; read the deal.
- **Aftermath:** lets the dawn come; says goodbye to Fibi.

### Comptroller Lucan Thale · Finale · Omen 50 (steel) · Balance 99,999,999
- **Phase 1, Regression to the Mean:** every wheel compresses toward 5 (faces far from 5 shrink each round). Counter: Cascades through 4-5-6.
- **Phase 2, Fixed Fate:** wheels stop spinning. A puzzle round: you **arrange** your heroes' digits instead of stopping them, with Thale removing one option per turn.
- **Phase 3, Zero:** NIL begins alone; recruited core companions automatically recall at authored phase milestones, including reserve members. Optional recruits add variants; absent recruits/faces do not block the win. The final guided ritual visibly provides legal choices and an optional 0-0-0 arrangement for resonance 0. Story §9 defines unlock/confirmation; no random last hit selects an ending.
- **Telegraph:** the Basilica's clock hands point at the face Thale will freeze.
- **Ultimate, *The Final Audit*:** the whole nave becomes a ledger; every number on screen is counted out loud.
- **Aftermath:** depends on the ending; in the true ending he tends the pigeons at the Open Hand shrine.

Phase safety: face shrink retains minimum readable exposure; any temporary removals preserve at least four legal faces including the chosen resonance and 0 (one protected face when these overlap). Phase 2's arrangement puzzle provides an explicit hand containing a legal winning configuration, previews the consequence of each removal and resets the hand after failure; it does not delete the last valid solution. Phase 3's guided ritual is not restricted by earlier removed faces. Phase thresholds, per-phase stakes summing 99,999,999, action intents and exact puzzle hands are content data that must be supplied and fixture-tested before the finale build is enabled; this outline does not certify those missing assets/data as implemented.

## 6. Expansion encounter enabling gate

Each future encounter requires a complete content record: legal wheels/widths, actor/target IDs, maximum stakes, phase thresholds/order, intent values, modifier lifetime/ordering, target/round semantics, terminal behavior, safe retry, text/audio/reduced-motion variants and same-land counters. Exact fixtures must cover transition/terminal overlap and every required legal party/digit; a named story requirement is not proof the encounter is ready. Unimplemented content tables/visuals and measured balance remain production work, distinct from the resolved narrative rules above. No extension silently imports an old prompt's obsolete boss mechanic.
