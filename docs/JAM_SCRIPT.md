# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Jam Script and Storyboards (Gate A8)

Every line in the jam slice, in the [`NARRATIVE_STYLE.md`](NARRATIVE_STYLE.md) format, plus shot-by-shot boards. Full campaign: [`STORY.md`](STORY.md). Lines are short; **NIL speaks least**. ## 2. Prologue

**Rain Alley 9 (S → E)**
> *Dawn. The Tally beam sweeps the alley. AMA's Omen ticks 03… 02… 01.*
> **TOBI:** Mum?
> **COLLECTOR:** Ama Okoro. Your account's closed.
> *A hooded figure steps between them. Above their head: NaN.*
> **COLLECTOR:** Move.
> *(Interactive: digits spin around Ama's 01. TAP. The wheel slams on 07.)*
> **COLLECTOR:** …That one has no number.
> **NIL:** Neither do you, now.  *(Beat.)*  Run.

**Naming shrine (S → E)**
> **BROTHER NOUGHT:** In here. Quick. *(He bolts the door.)* Every soul needs a number, child.
> **NIL:** I don't have one.
> **BROTHER NOUGHT:** Then pick. Out loud. The machine is listening.
> *(STATE YOUR LUCKY NUMBER.)*
> *Every screen in the city: REMAINDER DETECTED. The Basilica's eye opens.*
> **BROTHER NOUGHT:** *(quietly)* Hap's watching.

**Fight 1 barks:** NIL (stop): "Fourteen." · NIL (Jackpot): "…Huh." · NIL (hurt): "Noted."

**Vesper's rescue (S, 12 s)**
> *Three Debt Hounds circle NIL. A shot. A hat lands on the cobbles, then a woman lands beside it.*
> **VESPER:** Need a hand? Seven bucks.
> **NIL:** I don't have seven bucks.
> **VESPER:** Then you owe me. Everybody owes somebody, kid.

**Fight 2 tutorial lines (E, one line each):** "Tap a hero to choose who strikes." · "Shimmering faces finish a pattern." · *(after two Busts)* **VESPER:** "Feel that? Luck's turning. Hap's watching."

**Gus's stall (E)**
> **GUS:** Charms from seven countries, all genuine, most of them. First one's on credit.
> **VESPER:** Nothing's on credit with Gus.
> **GUS:** Everything's on credit with Gus. That's the business.

**Maeve's forge (S, optional)**
> **MAEVE:** Third time lucky! You're the third stranger today. The first two were Collectors.
> **MAEVE:** *(after the fight)* Right. I'm coming. Somebody has to fix your charms.

**Varro (E + S)**
> **VARRO:** NaN. Worth more than my whole street.
> **VESPER:** He's got a sister. Debt eats her Omen every morning.
> **VARRO:** Don't talk about her.
> *(Boss intro card: VARRO KADE · THE LEDGERHOUND · OMEN 61 · "He steals your last digit.")*
> **VARRO:** *(phase 2)* Final notice.
> **VESPER:** *(ultimate)* Seven chambers. Pick one.
> **VARRO:** *(defeated)* Go on. Settle me.
> **NIL:** No.
> **VARRO:** …You'll regret that. *(He limps away.)*

**Rooftop: the map reveal (S, 20 s)**
> **VESPER:** The Upper Gate wants seven Seal Digits. One from every land's Auditor. *(Six lands light up across the horizon.)*
> **NIL:** That's six.
> **VESPER:** The seventh's yours. The number you just told the whole city.
> *Varro's Seal shatters over the drain gates: 1.*

**Thale (S, 8 s)**
> *The Basilica nave. The Comptroller reads the alert and closes his eyes.*
> **THALE:** There you are.

**Hap's Dream 0 (V, 15 s, should)**
> *A void full of stars. A vast figure with dice for eyes, wearing a street fortune-teller's face, leans down.*
> **HAP:** Pick a number. Any number. *(It sees NIL's number.)* …Oh, *that* one. Interesting.
> *Dice rattle like laughter. NIL wakes.*

## 3. Act I

**Ferry (S)**
> **TETSU:** I don't ask where you're going. I used to ask. I used to work for the people who ask.

**Moon Pier barks:** HINA (later): "Hina approves!" · MAEVE: "Horseshoe goes open end UP." · VESPER: "Lucky seven, unlucky you."

**The Night Parade / Hina joins (V + S)**
> *The party hides inside the parade, disguised as yokai. Maeve's disguise is a hat.*
> *An umbrella hops out of a shrine stand, opens one eye, and becomes a small girl.*
> **HINA:** You smell like a hundred wishes.
> **NIL:** I've never made one.
> **HINA:** Then they're other people's. Hina likes other people's. *(She attaches herself to NIL's sleeve.)* Hina is coming.
> **VESPER:** Does it bite?
> **HINA:** *She* bites.

**Ofuku's shrine (E)**
> **OFUKU:** One eye when you wish. One eye when it comes true. I paint one a day, for whoever needs it.

**Teahouse bond scene: Vesper + Hina (S, should)**
> **HINA:** You flip that coin and never look. Why?
> **VESPER:** If I look, I have to believe it.
> **HINA:** Hina looks at everything. That's how Hina got real. Almost.

**Enma's trial (E, should)**
> **ENMA:** The living don't pass this gate. *(He opens his ledger. A page is missing.)* …You are not written here. You are not written anywhere.
> **ENMA:** *(after the trial)* Pass, then. Something unwritten should see what it's missing.

**Tamamo (E + V)**
> **TAMAMO:** Little NaN. Do you know what happens to a story nobody tells? It fades. Every fox and lantern on these islands is fading.
> **HINA:** So you sold us.
> **TAMAMO:** I *saved* us. Fixed Fate keeps everything.
> *(Boss intro card: TAMAMO · THE NINE-TAILED AUDITOR · OMEN 99 ×9 · "Her foxfire lies about your digits.")*
> **TAMAMO:** *(ultimate)* Hundred demons. One night.
> **HINA:** *(counter-ultimate)* Hina has lived a hundred years of nights. *(The umbrella opens its eye.)*
> **TAMAMO:** *(defeated)* What is a yokai worth, without belief?
> **NIL:** What's anyone worth without a number?
> *Tamamo laughs softly, touches Hina's head with one tail, and fades into the lanterns. Seal 9.*

**Ending (S)**
> *The Basilica. Thale watches a second Seal alarm.*
> **THALE:** The Remainder is learning to count.
> *Card: TO BE CONTINUED: THE RIVER EMPIRE.*
