# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Legal, IP and AI Policy (Gate B14)

Not legal advice: a checklist to review with a lawyer before the Steam page and any commercial release.

---

## 1. Originality
- **Inspired by, not copying.** Balatro (build-crafting structure), anime and Studio Trigger/Ghibli-era looks (art direction), the Cyberpunk 2077 art book (method of presentation) are influences only. No names, characters, art, music, UI layouts or text are reused.
- The Cyberpunk 2077 art book PDF used for study **is not committed** to the repo and is never redistributed.
- Folklore figures used (yokai, the Ten Courts, Chitragupta, the Moirai, the Headless Horseman) are public-domain traditions; our designs are original. Hap is our own invention.
- Run a **trademark search** on "ZERO-SUM: OMEN", "Hap" (as a product term) and character names before the Steam page.

## 2. Assets and licences
- Fonts: Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License (Big Shoulders Display, Barlow Semi Condensed, JetBrains Mono, Permanent Marker, Noto family). Keep licence files in `licenses/`.
- Audio: track every SFX library and its licence in `audio/LICENSES.md`; original music contracts give us full rights.
- Contractors and collaborators sign a work-for-hire or licence agreement before their work enters the repo.

## 3. AI policy
- **Disclose everything AI-assisted** in [`AI_DISCLOSURE.md`](AI_DISCLOSURE.md) and on store pages (jam rules require it; Steam requires a content survey disclosure).
- AI images and video are **drafts**: shipped art is overpainted by people. Prompts never name living artists or copyrighted characters.
- Check each AI tool's terms for commercial use and output ownership (Higgsfield, any Hugging Face model's licence, Meshy) and record them here before shipping AI-derived assets.
- No AI voice clones of real people. Any AI voice is disclosed and credited as such.

## 4. Gambling and ratings
- **No real-money gambling, no paid randomness, no loot boxes.** Casino imagery is aesthetic.
- Target ratings: ESRB T / PEGI 12 (fantasy violence, gambling themes). Answer rating questionnaires honestly about "simulated gambling" imagery.

## 5. Privacy
Telemetry opt-in only ([`TELEMETRY.md`](TELEMETRY.md)); privacy policy before any data collection; no data from minors knowingly collected.

## 6. Jam rules
Follow the Official Unity Game Jam 2026 rules: work made during the jam window, WebGL build, AI disclosed, theme "Lucky Number". Keep commit history as evidence of the timeline.
