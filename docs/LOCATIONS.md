# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Locations Guide

One page per land: what it is, how it looks, sounds and feels, and where the fights happen. Colour scripts and rendering rules are in [`ART_DIRECTION.md`](ART_DIRECTION.md) §4 and §8.

Updated 7 November 2026 under [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). **Specified locations, not accepted runtime stages.** Jam needs one coherent Meridian stage with Rain Alley/stall/drain-gate/rooftop lighting and prop variants; other district/stage lists are campaign targets. No new region is implied to be playable by a map illustration. Region culture is lived and internally varied, never a claim that all real people share one custom.

## Meridian Undercity (Prologue) and the Basilica (Finale)
- **Inspired by:** a cosmopolitan modern capital; the immigrant quarters of every great port city.
- **Geography:** a plateau city; the Undercity is stacked in the drains and cliffs beneath it, with the Basilica's tower rising from the plateau's heart.
- **Districts:** the Remainder Wards (Remainder + Charmpunk) · the Charm Bazaar (pure Charmpunk) · the Exchange Terraces (Gilded Fortune over Ledgerism) · the Basilica Ring (pure Ledgerism).
- **Key locations:** Rain Alley 9 (where NIL wakes) · the black-market Naming shrine · Maeve's forge in the Celtic quarter · the drain gates (Varro's boss fight) · the sealed Upper Gate · the Basilica nave (finale).
- **Little things:** charms of every culture over every door; laundry between tenements; balcony gardens (a garden is luck you can see); cats; hand-painted lucky numbers.
- **Sound:** rain, Nixie hum, distant Dawn Tally bells, street musicians, Collector sirens.
- **Battle stages:** Rain Alley · the drain gates · the Bazaar crossroads · the Basilica nave.
- **Reel object:** a dead three-tube Nixie sign.
- **Human conflict / decision:** protect a specific neighbor under a ranked economy; the confiscation reserve visibly funds Ama's six-unit rescue. A low-Omen mechanic disagrees with a high-Omen clerk about what makes a safe staircase.
- **Ordinary life:** laundry borrowed across balconies, a late noodle delivery, a cat that ignores every lucky charm. Ama and Tobi are visibly safe at the jam rooftop; campaign letters show consequences later.

## Hyakki (百鬼), the Isles of a Hundred Demons (Act I)
- **Inspired by:** Japanese folklore (yokai, tsukumogami, the Seven Lucky Gods, Enma); ukiyo-e and Edo-period towns.
- **Geography:** a chain of volcanic islands, cedar forests, terraced fishing towns, torii gates on every hill.
- **Culture:** humans and yokai share festivals; numbers 4 (*shi*) and 9 (*ku*) are avoided; 7 and 8 are blessed.
- **Key locations:** Moon Pier (arrival; battle stage) · the Night Parade bridge · the Shrine of the Seven Lucky Gods · the kappa river · Enma's gate of the dead (keeper trial) · Tamamo's foxfire palace (boss).
- **Little things:** daruma dolls with one eye painted; beckoning cats; fireflies; wind chimes; one tired kappa selling cucumbers.
- **Sound:** shamisen, shakuhachi, taiko for battle, cicadas and wind chimes.
- **Meridian intrusion:** a Ledgerist toll gate on the shrine road; Collectors in the harbour.
- **Reel object:** a torii with three hanging lanterns.
- **Human conflict / decision:** belonging versus being preserved unchanged; locals disagree whether a parade costume should be repaired or remade. Hina can be real at 99 without proving age confers worth.
- **Ordinary life:** a ferryman arguing over yesterday's fish price, islanders who treat charms as family keepsakes rather than guarantees. Maeve's campaign caravan and Ofuku's letter support recoverable bonds; no runtime cultural-tour lecture.

## The River Empire: Bāfā Harbor (八发港) and the Ten Courts (十殿) (Act II)
- **Inspired by:** Chinese folk religion, festival culture and river trade.
- **Geography:** a great golden river delta; the harbor city of Bāfā on its banks; the river's end falls into the underworld of the Ten Courts.
- **Key locations:** the Lantern Festival · the Luck Exchange (live Omen price boards) · Jinling's pawnshop · the river's end · Naihe Bridge and Meng Po's pavilion · the Hall of the Book (Judge Cui's trial) · Jin Chan's coin palace (boss).
- **Little things:** red envelopes, mahjong tables, dragon boats, joss paper drifting into the river, lucky 8 s on every price tag.
- **Sound:** erhu, guzheng, firecrackers, abacus clicks; the underworld is silent except for brush strokes.
- **Meridian intrusion:** the Exchange is half Basilica office, half temple.
- **Reel object:** a golden abacus (harbor); a floating ghost ledger (underworld).
- **Human conflict / decision:** debt ownership versus accountable repair. Merchants disagree with both Jin Chan's bank and Jinling's proposed relief; not every trader is greedy or every debtor helpless.
- **Ordinary life:** dragon-boat repair, shared festival meals, a messenger who wants a quiet day. Archive copies survive enforcement-book burning. The true account and missing dead-count record cause the journey to the observatory.

## Śūnyapura, the City Where Zero Was Born (Act III)
- **Inspired by:** Indian mythology, astronomy and mathematics; stepwells; open-air stone observatories.
- **Geography:** a monsoon plain; a city built around a colossal stone observatory; deep geometric stepwells.
- **Key locations:** Great Observatory · Stepwell of a Thousand Steps · Chitragupta's nonlethal trial Hall · Vetāla banyan · plateau with invented Basilica Eclipse Engines “Rahu” and “Ketu”. Celestial figures are not enemy assets.
- **Little things:** a lemon and seven chillies over every door; rangoli patterns; chai stalls; pigeons; monsoon puddles mirroring the instruments.
- **Sound:** sitar, tabla, temple bells, rain on stone.
- **Meridian intrusion:** Basilica surveyors re-measuring the observatory's numbers.
- **Reel object:** three sundial dials.
- **Human conflict / decision:** knowledge about a person requires consent. A surveyor, student and diver disagree about what counts as evidence; Shunya learns that proving NIL's origin does not entitle her to publish it.
- **Ordinary life:** wet manuscripts drying over chai, a stepped-well maintenance queue, a teacher who loses his own spectacles. The silent post-reveal steps are a playable rest beat. Machines use bolted discs/rails/operator stamps, never sacred head/body silhouettes.

## Kroton Nova, the Sieve of the Aegean (Act IV)
- **Inspired by:** Greek mythology and Pythagorean number mysticism.
- **Geography:** a white island city built as a vast stone sieve over a blue sea; the cave of the Moirai beneath.
- **Key locations:** the Agora of Primes · the Sieve terraces · the undercity of the fallen (Remainder shanties) · the Moirai's cave (three trials) · the Archon's temple (boss).
- **Little things:** blue evil-eye beads; olive trees; goats on the terraces; mosaics of numbers.
- **Sound:** lyre, bouzouki, waves, chanting of primes.
- **Meridian intrusion:** the undercity of the "composite" people built from Basilica scrap.
- **Reel object:** three marble columns with mosaic numerals.
- **Human conflict / decision:** purity of a definition versus protecting people. Surface citizens argue against the Synod too; Kallisto's undercity contains different beliefs and professions, not one interchangeable victim group.
- **Ordinary life:** children repairing a ladder, olive harvest complaints, goats interfering with an official speech. The Archon's fall is a damaged mechanism giving way, not his Civic 17 magically changing into a composite because Audit health fell.

## The Crossroads Territory (Act V)
- **Inspired by:** American folklore: frontier tall tales, the blues crossroads legend, immigrant charms, desert highways and casinos.
- **Geography:** a desert plateau crossed by highways; the casino boomtown of Lucky Strike, where the same night repeats.
- **Key locations:** the midnight crossroads (the deal) · Route 7 motels · the Ghost Train · the Hollow (the Headless Horseman) · the House's casino (boss).
- **Little things:** horseshoes over doors (open end up); rabbit's-foot keychains; lucky pennies on the railway; diners; neon cacti.
- **Sound:** slide guitar, harmonica, a slot-machine choir, coyotes.
- **Meridian intrusion:** the House finances recent Basilica operations; in return a Basilica contract powers the casino's protection loop. This reciprocal pact did not construct the centuries-old Basilica. Collectors deal blackjack.
- **Reel object:** a giant slot machine.
- **Human conflict / decision:** protecting someone can become refusing their right to change. Fibi predicts repeated local routines, not prior meetings with newly arriving NIL. She consents to cancelling the fatal contract.
- **Ordinary life:** a diner changing a recipe, motel plumbing, a sheriff tired of an identical joke. Iteration 1,597 follows 1,596 completed local repeats; outside time advances only the anchored night. Motel, contract and dawn supply Fibi's four pre-finale stages.
