## 1. The visual promise

Precise ink makes the world convincing; a clear decision makes the action exciting. The reader should recognize the logic of a decoder, a coat repair or a confiscation clamp before the same object changes a fight. Large, readable shapes carry the first impression. Close inspection reveals work, status, history and personality.

Use an original combination of detailed mechanical drawing, expressive adult anatomy, controlled crosshatching, selective halftone, matte painted depth and sharply staged action. Study the broad craft of dramatic adventure manga and its contrast between spectacle and deadpan humour. Do not reproduce an artist's characteristic rendering, copied panel, franchise likeness, signature composition or existing character design. Image prompts describe the actual visual traits and do not name an artist or franchise.

The fresh v5 NIL/Vesper/Varro designs and corrected cast sheets are identity references for the new series; Mangaka’s recurring cast must preserve them. Older glossy concepts and simplified figures are historical studies, not interchangeable costumes. The shared design story is visible luck machinery and the people living under it: repaired actuary clothing, dealer hardware, paper records, human hands, worn glass and heavy brass. Random jewellery and generic fantasy armour do not substitute for specificity.

## 3. Black-and-white and emotional registers

**Dramatic register:** ink masses, measured perspective, weight in hands and planted feet, purposeful material detail. Separate Wards rain/windows and NIL/Vesper/Varro through black/white masses, line, material and selected tone. NIL’s orange hair lock becomes a consistent marked lock in B&W. Colour references govern separate covers/inserts, not story-page comprehension.

**Impact register:** anticipation, a readable path, one decisive contact and aftermath. A still high-contrast black/white panel may emphasize the important hit. In the game reader it remains static. Reduced-motion adaptation uses a static crop or crossfade; never require strobing or colour-step flashing to understand an outcome.

**Comedy register:** a held expression, a wide silence, fewer background marks or an ordinary object refusing to cooperate. Characters retain their adult proportions and identity. The joke comes after a serious beat, not across a mother's fear or Varro's confession. No copied gag face, cultural caricature or constant deflation of sincere stakes.

**Quiet register:** let the page breathe after force. Rain on a latch, a hand releasing a coin, or a warm window is enough. The rooftop must feel earned after the boss, not like another explosion announcing a sequel.

## 4. Action must remain understandable

1. Establish the floor, exits and participants before tightening the camera. In the drain-gate sequence, NIL/Vesper begin left and Varro right. Reverse the axis only after a wide or overhead re-establishing panel.
2. Separate wind-up, path, contact and reaction. A smear cannot replace the pose that explains an action. Keep silhouette separation between arm, weapon and torso.
3. Show the useful object. The captured digit moves from scoring slot3 to the jar in the same panel; the rescue jar and Ama's credited Civic frame share a panel. Power is not a glow with no visible cause.
4. Respect weight. Varro's hook commits his shoulders and planted boots. Vesper's small recoil and coat tails differ from NIL's economical decoder movement. Debt Hounds are articulated machines, not injured real animals.
5. Stop spectacle when the rule needs reading. Pattern Sight marks legal completions; it does not secretly land the perfect face. The jar release track remains comprehensible during the attack.
6. Spend the largest panel on a changed situation. The settlement and the mercy are different climaxes: one shows force, the next shows choice.

- Resonance, Civic Omen, Audit Omen and Balance remain distinct. NIL's `NaN` identity stays visible. The chosen0 is valid and is never depicted as a healthy Civic00.

- The rooftop map shows six Seal slots and NIL's resonance as the seventh lock. Only Varro's first Seal has been acquired; the remaining five are campaign goals. The jam does not contain the outer regions.

**Reader mode:** a future read-only collectible with spoiler-aware chapter unlocks. Forward/back/bookmarks never grant rewards, change resonance, damage an enemy, release a Seal or overwrite a checkpoint. **Future cutscene mode:** the adapter pauses at live Naming, charm and combat gates, resumes only from actual completed gameplay, and uses the player's accepted values/results. The adapter's scene mapping is a specification, not an implemented Unity reader.
