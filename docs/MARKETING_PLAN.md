## 2. Jam capture pack

Capture the real Unity build at its tested resolution. Required shots: Rain Alley rescue with visible transfer; Naming/NaN reveal; two-hero stop and Pattern Sight; one intelligible charm effect; Varro jar/defence; rooftop ending. Include settings/control information and accurate AI-use/asset credits. Generated key art may decorate the page, but must not be labelled a gameplay screenshot.

Keep captures spoiler-light: Hap's cage, NIL's origin, later bosses and the true ending belong to the full campaign. Do not use Hyakki/Tamamo, a Link Art or a five-tube reel to imply those are in the jam.

Prepare a small feedback form matching PLAYTEST, accessibility notes and reproducible bug-report fields. Screenshot-friendly scores can support sharing without a live service. Daily seeds, leaderboards and a Discord community are optional post-jam work with moderation/service costs; do not promise them on the jam page.
