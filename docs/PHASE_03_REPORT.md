## Decisions and corrections

Penny's retained tokens are nonspendable counters; cancellation discharges the trapping obligation and a marker can record release. A new magical coin is not required. Niko's competence is route knowledge under adult supervision; repeated falls through dangerous machinery are not a standing joke. Nought knows an outlawed tradition, not the physical cage's location. Gus's correspondence and dispatch work do not require teleportation between hubs.

Livia chooses care and can reject Varro's proposed price. Mara has work, education and a family outside Vesper's redemption. Jinling's funding distinguishes distributable surplus from deposits and wages. Shunya preserves a failed model and permissions for data use. Prima's rescue earns participation and a hearing, not absolution. Hap's freedom does not grant unaccountable divine authority.

The resident organizations are provisional and limited before the climax; the year-later epilogue carries longer-term representative selection and continuing work. No country or faith is represented by a unanimous single opinion. Existing game-only recruits/archetypes have explicit optional scope rather than being silently added as proof prerequisites. Future introduction of one requires a scene-ledger change.
