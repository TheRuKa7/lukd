# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Player Experience, Loops and Emotional Arc (Gate B1)

What the player should feel, minute by minute and act by act, and the loops that make "one more" irresistible without manipulation.

---

## 1. The three loops

| Loop | Length | What happens | The pull |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The tap** | 3 s | Choose a striker → stop the wheel → digit slams into the reel | Skill + suspense, every three seconds |
| **The round / fight** | 10 s / 60–90 s | Three digits → a pattern → the count-up → the enemy's turn → parry | A small payoff every round, a big one most fights |
| **The run / act** | 25–35 min | Map choices → charms, faces, gems → companions and bonds → the boss | The build: "what if I add *this*?" |
| **The journey** | 6–8 h | Lands, story, bond arcs, NIL's verbs, the reveal, the endings | Mystery and people you care about |
| **The ledger** (post-game) | endless | Endless Ledger, Omen Levels, Daily Omen, Hap's Gallery | Mastery and discovery |

## 2. Why it's moreish (and where we draw the line)

| Driver | How we use it | The line we don't cross |
|---|---|---|
| **Variable reward** | The reel's pattern odds; the Jackpot is rare enough to chase | No real-money randomness, ever; the casino is aesthetic only (World Bible §10) |
| **Near miss** | Two matching digits and a third that *almost* landed are shown as "so close" | We never fake a near miss; the wheel is honest and Pattern Sight shows real odds |
| **Agency over luck** | Every tap is a choice; the third striker reads the reel; builds bend the odds | Luck never decides a fight without a player decision first |
| **Pity / luck turns** | Hap's Favour: three Busts in a row can't happen | — |
| **Build discovery** | Synergies between digits, charms, faces and gems (Balatro's "oh, *that* works") | Every rule is readable; no hidden maths |
| **Collection** | 63 charms, Hap's faces, Codex entries, Link Arts | No completion that requires grinding |
| **Short sessions** | Saves at every node; a fight is 60–90 s | No energy timers, no daily login pressure |
| **Social** | Daily Omen seed and leaderboard; screenshot-friendly big numbers | Opt-in only |
| **Story pull** | Every act ends on a Thale scene and a Hap's Dream question | Cliffhangers resolve; no paid endings |

## 3. Emotional arc of the campaign

| Act | Target emotion | Intensity (1–10) | Rest beat | Peak beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue | Curiosity → thrill | 4 → 7 | Rooftop map reveal | REMAINDER DETECTED; Vesper's Requiem |
| I · Hyakki | Wonder, mischief | 5 → 8 | Teahouse with Hina | Hundred Demons' Night vs Hundred-Year Night |
| II · River Empire | Ambition, injustice | 6 → 8 | The silent Ten Courts | Jinling burns the books; Jin Chan's fall |
| III · Śūnyapura | Awe, identity shock | 4 → 9 | The quietest Teahouse (after the reveal) | "NIL is the Remainder"; the eclipse |
| IV · Kroton Nova | Moral anger → resolve | 6 → 8 | The undercity of the fallen | Prima holds the children over the drop |
| V · Crossroads | Fatalism → hope | 5 → 9 | The motel at 3 a.m., the 1,597th time | Dawn breaks |
| Finale | Dread → catharsis | 7 → 10 → 3 | The last campfire | Phase 3: every companion returns; the Open Hand |

**Intensity rules:** never more than two acts in a row peaking at 9+; every act has one scene with no music; every boss is followed by a quiet scene.

## 4. Moment-to-moment feelings (one fight)
Anticipation (enemy reveal and its rule) → control (choosing strikers) → suspense (the spin) → payoff (the stop, the pattern) → threat (the enemy wheel) → relief (parry) → triumph (the count-up and Settle). If any of these is missing for most testers, the fight is redesigned.

## 5. Player types and what each gets
| Player | Wants | Gets |
|---|---|---|
| **The story player** | Characters and the mystery | Assist mode, fast cinematics off, bond arcs, Hap's Dreams |
| **The build player** | Synergies and big numbers | 63 charms, gems, faces, Deals; the Endless Ledger |
| **The skill player** | Timing and mastery | Fast heroes, Omen Levels, Daily Omen leaderboard |
| **The collector** | Completion | Codex, Hap's Gallery, all Link Arts, secret recruits |
| **The jam rater** | A great 20 minutes | The First 10 Minutes plan; the full slice in 25–35 min |

**Done when:** each act in ACT_DESIGN has a named rest beat and peak beat, and playtests confirm the peak for most testers.
