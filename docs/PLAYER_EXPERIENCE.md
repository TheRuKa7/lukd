# ZERO-SUM: OMEN - Player Experience and Emotional Arc

Updated 7 November 2026. Intended experiences and pacing hypotheses; no player study is implied. [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) separates the jam from the full campaign and governs rules.

A stop is one action; a round, fight and act are not all called a run. Campaign act checkpoints and Endless Ledger reset policies are separate in ECONOMY_PROGRESSION. Cold download time is not the Start-to-first-action clock.

## 2. Agency and retention boundaries

- The wheel shows accessible faces and actual accepted outcomes. Pattern Sight offers information, not a fabricated near miss. Width is exposure under specified motion, not a measured player probability.
- Favour visibly repairs a would-be Bust after two consecutive Busts, before scoring/effects; it is not a hidden result label. Black Cat shares that conversion pipeline. Advanced challenge exceptions must be explicitly shown.
- No real-money randomness, energy timers, paid endings or daily-login pressure. Collecting is optional. Build power and surprise should follow readable rules rather than manipulative copy.
- Story players can use assists and shorter presentation. Skill players can pursue mastery without receiving exclusive main-story truth. Every chosen digit remains finishable; equal Overwrite multiplier does not mean identical early partner access or proven equal difficulty.
- Failure preserves a clear retry/checkpoint policy. The jam retries an encounter; later campaign rollback uses the complete act snapshot. Do not promise persistent rewards that are duplicated by a rollback.

## 4. Comprehension and relationships

The first tap target is20s from Start; lucky choice60s; detection before3min. Teach a pattern, then a defence, then a companion/order decision, then one charm. The required Varro counter works without optional recruits and has a round-based release fallback. A clear local resolution earns the rooftop teaser.

Narrative bonds express shared task, disagreement, costly choice and changed habit. Mathematical bonds express digit relations and are shown with concrete previews. Reserve/catch-up paths prevent a story-critical companion or late bond from silently blocking an ending. The pre-finale journal explains prerequisites and offers a return opportunity.

For each session record what the player tried, what they thought happened, time spent waiting and whether they chose to retry/continue. Automated path completion and AI critique are useful technical evidence; they do not establish delight, emotional recall or cultural approval. Thresholds in PLAYTEST trigger redesign, not claims of statistically proven demand.

Done when the candidate meets the technical comprehension gates and observed fresh-player feedback supports or corrects these hypotheses. Until then, intended emotions are specified, not verified.
