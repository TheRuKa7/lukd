# Pre-production plan: ten phases to a game-ready project

Adopted 9 November 2026. This plan covers everything that must exist **before game development starts**: the finished story, the cast and world designs, the concept artbook, all seven manga volumes, the cutscene and dialogue scripts, the anime series plan, Unity-ready 2D and 3D assets, the complete mechanics and UI/UX design, and a configured Unity project. It supersedes phases 5–10 of the [earlier ten-phase plan](TEN_PHASE_PLAN.md). The old phases 1–4 (baseline, scene ledger, character and society development, Volume 1 prose) are complete and carry over. The reasons behind each phase are in the [critique](CRITIQUE_2026-11-09.md).

## Principles

1. **One source of truth.** Every character, place, power, prop and line of dialogue has exactly one authoritative record. Generated documents, PDFs and Unity data are built from it, never edited by hand.
2. **Output over paperwork.** Checks guard continuity and data contracts. A phase report states what was made, what was verified and what is open, in under a page. Rule-keeping stays in the data and out of the prose.
3. **Cheap tools first, paid tools where they pay.** Blender and code are free and unlimited. Higgsfield and Meshy credits are spent on final-quality assets for the characters and places the player sees most. Every paid batch gets a priced test of 1–3 items before it scales.
4. **Tiers decide effort.** Each character and location gets a tier (below), and the tier decides how much art, 3D and dialogue it receives.
5. **Parallel where independent.** The novel is the critical path. Design, mechanics and Unity setup run beside it once the canon they depend on is locked.

## Order and parallel tracks

```
1 ──► 2 ──► 3 ──► 7 ──► 8 (volume by volume)
│                 └───► 9
├──► 4 (Volumes 1–3 cast now, the rest after 2) ──► 10 (assets)
├──► 5 ───────────────────────────────────────────► 10 (environments)
└──► 6 ──────────────────────────────────────────► 10 (project, data, UI)
```

Phase 1 unlocks everything. The novel (2 → 3) is the critical path. Phases 4, 5 and 6 start as soon as phase 1 is done, beginning with the cast and regions of Volumes 1–3, which are already written. Phase 10's project setup and data pipeline can start straight after phase 6's data contracts; its asset import waits for phases 4–5. Manga production starts with Volume 1 once phase 3 locks the text.

## The Unity jam decision (required in phase 1)

The Official Unity Game Jam 2026 closes on **14 November 2026 at 17:30 IST**, five days from this plan. Its Prologue slice is already fully specified ([JAM_SLICE](JAM_SLICE.md), [JAM_SCRIPT](JAM_SCRIPT.md)) and the scoring core exists in the prototype. This ten-phase plan puts game development after pre-production, so it gives up the jam unless one of these is chosen:

- **Skip the jam.** Follow this plan as written.
- **Run a parallel jam sprint.** A separate, time-boxed track builds the 10–15 minute Meridian Prologue in Unity with existing concept art and placeholder sprites, then stops on 14 November. It does not change canon, and its project is not the phase 10 project.

**Decision, 9 November 2026: run the parallel jam sprint.** The playable build, its tests and the itch.io page draft are in [jam/README.md](../jam/README.md).
