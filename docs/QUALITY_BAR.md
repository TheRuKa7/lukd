# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Quality Bar and Definition of Done (Gate A10)

"GOTY quality" has to mean a checklist, or it means nothing. A feature is **done** only when it passes its checklist **and** has been playtested.

---

## 0. The trailer test (applies to everything)
Would a 3-second clip of this make a stranger click? If not, it isn't done.

## 1. A fight
- [ ] Teaches or tests exactly one idea (its enemy rule or Land Law)
- [ ] Hits its round target (TUNING §1) for an average tester
- [ ] Every special move is telegraphed one round ahead
- [ ] The stop, hit, pattern and Settle feel match GAME_FEEL §2–§4
- [ ] At least one moment worth a GIF
- [ ] No input blocked for more than 3 s without a skip prompt

## 2. A boss
- [ ] Its rule is explained in one line on its intro card
- [ ] Two or more phases, each visibly different
- [ ] A cinematic ultimate, and a counter the player can find in the same land
- [ ] Its defeat changes the world (story beat) and the music resolves

## 3. A cutscene
- [ ] Storyboarded and timed before production
- [ ] Skippable after first view; subtitled; speaker names shown
- [ ] No line longer than 15 words (NIL: shorter)
- [ ] One image in it is good enough for the store page

## 4. A character (art)
- [ ] Reads as a black silhouette at 64 px (the art book's readability page)
- [ ] Signature colour is the most saturated thing on them
- [ ] Two tones + one rim; coloured variable-weight lines; no airbrush
- [ ] Every canon lock respected (Character Bible)
- [ ] Hands, faces and prop handedness correct after overpaint
- [ ] Cultural details checked against the World Bible §10 and the consultation log

## 5. A background
- [ ] One light source and a time of day; atmospheric depth
- [ ] 2–3 deliberate "little things" (Locations Guide)
- [ ] Neon only where the fiction allows
- [ ] The battle floor is clear and readable behind actors
- [ ] Split into parallax layers per ASSET_LIST

## 6. A UI screen
- [ ] It's an object from the world (UX_SPEC §3)
- [ ] Answers its questions at a glance; two lines of text max
- [ ] Works with mouse, touch and keyboard; meets the text-size minimums
- [ ] Every colour cue has a shape or number

## 7. A sound
- [ ] Recognisable with eyes closed
- [ ] Sits right in the mix (AUDIO §5); has a reduced-intensity variant
- [ ] Licensed and logged

## 8. Never ship
Placeholder text · stray AI artefacts (wrong digits, "Nan" for "NaN", broken hands) · unreviewed cultural details · flashes that fail the photosensitivity check · a crash or soft-lock in the critical path · an undisclosed AI asset.

## 9. Reviews
- **Daily:** each owner checks their finished items against this list at stand-up.
- **Tue 10 (feature freeze):** whole-team review of every Must feature; anything failing is fixed or cut.
- **Thu 12 (release candidate):** "never ship" sweep.

**Done when:** the team uses this list in the Tue 10 feature-freeze review.
