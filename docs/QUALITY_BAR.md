# ZERO-SUM: OMEN — Quality bar and definition of done

Updated 7 November 2026 IST. Quality means accepted behaviour and evidence, not a claim of AAAcompletion. Written specs are specified; implemented code/assets require verification; player response requires actual observation. See PROJECT_STATUS and DECISIONS.

## 0. Small complete experience

Every required piece supports the Prologue's readable stop→pattern→consequence and NIL/Vesper/Varro story. A 3-second trailer clip is useful marketing review, not a release acceptance test for saves, controls or quiet story scenes. No new region or charged ultimate enters before its gate.

## 1. Audit and boss

A complete Audit accepts only eligible inputs, latches displayed faces, resolves exactrules/bonuses once, shows actual versus scored digits, and stops on terminal state. Enemyintent/counter is understandable; Burst/Pattern/Favour feedback reconciles with Balance. Test all required chosen digits and input modes. Fightlengthtargets need actual observations.

Varro shows jarstorage/substitution/expiry/restoration and phase warning; counters work without optional heroes. Affectedplayerroundexpiry prevents lock when enemyturns skip. Victory changes the story; stake settlement does not silently kill a surviving character. Futurebosses have separate BESTIARY/F-KIT acceptance before enable.

## 2. Character and environment acceptance

Character: recognizable silhouette at gameplay scale, consistent canonprops/handedness/colour, correct Na N/digits layered as text, no broken anatomy/AIartefacts in finalframes, animation key poses and stable identity acrossshots. A mirrored front is not a real back turnaround. Candidateart is not accepted merely because it renders in a book.

Environment: one coherent light/perspective/material language, readable battlefloor and semantic UI, a few authored worlddetails, consistent parallax/depth. Validate in the actual camera withactors/VFX rather than approving only a standalone painting. Generated/local/scripted fallbacks follow the samebar. AI processing replaces required human overpaint/rigging stages under D 02; no paid generation is required.

## 3. UI and narrative

Requiredscreenflow works withkeyboard/mouse/touch, readable focus/labels/text scale, pause/skip, and distinct resonance/Civic/Auditconcepts. Storytext is user-paced, subtitled and speaker-labelled; short dialogue targets do not override natural meaning or accessibility. Cutsceneerror/skip preserves correct flags and leadsforward. Requiredgameplay feedback is inengine; optional video hasbundled URL/still fallback.

## 4. Audio, motion and provenance

Audio agrees withresult/counter, is intelligible muted through visual cues, has volume controls and survives browser unlock/resume. Reduced Motion disables flash/shake/slowmo without changing outcome. Finaleffect/cinematic captures need actual photosensitivity analysis evidence; no blanket certificate is claimed. Everyincludedasset has source/tool/version/licence/provenance and AIuse disclosure; blockedterms/unverified external rights mustbe resolved or theasset replacedby a documented owned/local fallback.

## 5. Never mark complete without evidence

No crash/soft-lock/dataloss or wrong critical rule; no misleading accepted face/damage; no fake playtests/performance/cultural approval; no unlicensed/unlogged asset or undisclosed AIuse; no unfinished future feature exposed in menus. Cultural AIresearch has scope/uncertainty; it doesnot standfor community approval. Releaseclaims mustmatchthe actualchecks and projectowner's documented disposition of remaining gaps.

## 6. Acceptance record

For each required feature/asset: owner, stable ID, relevant contract, implementation artifact, exact automated/manualcheck, build/hash, platform/capture, findings and disposition. Automatedreference checks currently cover rules, not Unitybuild/performance/full UX. Releasegate comes from QA_TEST_PLAN/JAM_SLICE after those artifacts exist. Targets 10 Novfreeze/11 Novlock/12 Novverification areplanned, not completed reviews.
