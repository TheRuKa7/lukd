## 1. The four numbers and the thing they measure

Luck is a real capacity for events to favor someone. It cannot make a broken bridge sound or replace a surgeon's knowledge. It can tilt whether a failing bolt gives way during a crossing, whether a doctor notices a symptom in time, or whether a letter reaches the right person. People can influence events through skill even with little luck; high luck is neither virtue nor competence.

| Record | What it measures | Where it appears | What it never means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civic Omen, 00–99 | Registered personal allocation in the merged Ledger | An identity aura and civic records; NIL instead has NaN | Combat HP, resonance, a moral score or a safe diagnosis |
| Resonance, 0–9 | The digit through which an Auditor most readily shapes an event | Naming record, personal practice and wheel bias | An allocation; Shunya's resonance 0 does not make her Settled |
| Balance | A contract's scaled stake/capacity within an Audit | Encounter account; the party shares a stake | Spendable fen or a direct one-for-one Civic Omen increase |
| Audit Omen, 99–00 | The share of a positive maximum Balance remaining | A separate combat frame; NaN remains visible over NIL | A replacement Civic identity or automatic death when it reaches 00 |

Each Audit defines its accounting scale before it begins. A score of a million Audit units does not create a million luck-ins, and an exhausted guard or machine stake does not grant the winner the opponent's Civic allocation. A registered transfer requires an explicit source, destination, consent or seizure authority, amount and receipt. This distinction allows astronomical combat payoffs without turning every fight into unlimited currency generation.

Under the Basilica, luck is conserved across registered people, reserves and the hidden Remainder account. The Rounding moves fractions into an unlisted account; they do not cease to exist. The prologue makes the rule visible: six confiscated units leave the jar as Ama's Civic 01 becomes 07. NIL changes a route, not the total.

### An account that can be filmed

A legitimate luck transfer has five visible beats: the operator names the source; both meters show the intended amount; a single channel opens; the source loses exactly what the recipient gains; the receipt closes the transaction. For an illicit seizure the consent seal is missing, not the conservation step. For a fake receipt the brass stamp fails to mate with the jar. Use these actions in panels and animations instead of an exposition speech.

The Civic aura is real; its Nixie tube, embroidered numeral or steel badge is a social amplification of it. A poor resident can hide or obscure a display but cannot become unnumbered by losing a necklace. Only NIL's identity is unregistered NaN. A damaged tube can misreport, and the disagreement between aura, tube and paper record is useful evidence.

## 2. What a power is

Auditing is the trained ability to expose a possible event as a wheel and stop its outcome. The school changes the instrument, permitted transformation and habit of thought. It does not exempt the practitioner from costs, material limits or another person's consent. Civilian charm use and ordinary craft are not equivalent to consciously stopping a wheel.

| School | Source and instrument | Practical operation | Cost and failure | Counter and visual evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger Auditing | Registered contracts, brass wrist-wheels, jars and seals | Names a claim, locks an amount, routes a permitted transfer or temporary digit debt | Requires a valid account/channel; worn gears jam; a false license creates a disputable seizure | Break/expire the declared debt condition; show the jar's latch and receipt rather than an invisible debuff |
| Shrine Binding | Cared-for objects, declared vows, paper wards and local memory | Attaches a bounded promise to a charm or object; repairs relationships between owner, object and place | A damaged vessel or contradictory promise destabilizes it; repeated attention cannot manufacture infinite luck | Read the vow, repair or release it; paper edges, lacquer repairs and a separate ward ribbon communicate state |
| Brush Writing | A verified record, witness copies, ink and seals | Amends a recorded digit through an authorized rewrite; compares living and dead accounts | Ink and access are finite; a correction is recorded and cannot secretly erase its own debit | Preserve the counter-copy, force the operator to show both versions; wet/dry ink and broken seals show changes |
| Karma Weaving | A ledger of deeds and returns, instruments and measured intervals | Banks declared defensive/Zero-Sum outcomes; spends a bounded resource to redirect a later event | Gameplay caps and spend rules are in SYSTEMS; in-world practice needs a recorded act, not a moral verdict on a person | Deny the trigger or exhaust the declared budget; a tied geometric thread opens when spent |
| Sieve Geometry | Number relations, measured apertures, thread and engineered shields | Selects valid numerical structures; excludes or factors a permitted face/strike | Its constraints also exclude useful choices; an imperfect physical sieve can fail regardless of doctrine | Use the permitted composite/prime counter, protect a removed option or expose a flawed premise; shutters and holes visibly move |
| Dealing | A contract, informed stakes, a deck or Black Book and witnesses | Exchanges a declared benefit for a declared restriction; limited loops preserve only named records | The exact maintenance clause matters; wagers can be declined; a loop is local, not universal time control | Expose/cancel the clause, change retained knowledge or satisfy an exit condition; watch, contract and reset markers agree |
| Remainder practice | NIL's fused decoder and access to the unlisted fractions | Splice, Carry, Rewrite and later verbs reconnect routes the ordinary system discards | NIL is a person, not an unlimited reservoir; each verb follows its SYSTEMS unlock and payload | Fixed channels constrain them; the interrupted ring and mismatched records show what was rerouted |

### The limits that keep action intelligible

Powers need a subject, an instrument or vessel, a trigger, a boundary and an aftermath. A fan can launch its physical beads through a luck-shaped route; it does not summon metal from nowhere. A revolver still needs a chamber and ammunition. A protective charm changes its declared event; it does not make cloth bulletproof in every scene. Foxfire can mislead an image, but the player must see both accepted and resolved information before commitment when gameplay depends on the distinction.

The six regional schools are traditions with internal disagreements. They are not six national personalities, elemental races or disguised avatars of Hap. Local keepers and revered figures retain their own identities. The Basilica's claim that only its notation is legitimate is political doctrine, not objective proof that every other practice is the same religion.

### How an Auditor learns

Apprentices first keep records without stopping anything. They then stop an inert training wheel, name a single outcome and demonstrate a safe cancellation. Qualification requires showing the source and return of a stake, recognizing an invalid face, and ending an Audit without harming a bystander. Meridian requires a license; regional traditions certify their own practitioners. A local expert can therefore be skilled and unlicensed in Meridian. Children perform Naming at seven, but Naming is not combat certification.

Failure leaves a trace: a bruised wrist, cracked glass, spent ink, a snapped thread or a cancelled contract. Rest repairs concentration; trained craft repairs equipment. There is no generic glowing aura that proves somebody has secretly become stronger.

## 3. A day under the Dawn Tally

**Before sunrise:** bell crews inspect ropes and the public reserve clerk posts yesterday's balance. Bakers work from stored fuel and flour; power has not replaced food. Tube repairers leave dirty gloves outside the clinic. Residents with low allocations travel in groups because protection can be organized even when it cannot be bought.

**Dawn:** the Basilica beam checks registered accounts. Clocks strike zero. Allocations, authorized transfers and rounded fractions are processed; the Rounding's hidden destination is not publicly disclosed. A shuttered tube shop displays the before/after reading with a mechanical pointer. Civic numbers do not jump merely because someone lost a street fight.

**First hour:** Collectors execute warrants. An honest clerk reads the source account and appeal period aloud; a predatory one rushes the recipient past the consent plate. Schools open a safe waiting yard so children are not trapped in lift queues during collections.

**Second bell through afternoon:** stalls open, lifts carry workers to the plateau, and the Bazaar's runners move goods between cultures and districts. The Exchange issues price boards, but a noodle seller still needs cabbage delivered. Rain forces the mechanic and ferryman to solve ordinary problems with rope and spare parts.

**Evening:** the district clinic reconciles reserve transfers, a block kitchen lists tomorrow's needs, and shrine keepers repair the day's damaged objects. The wealthy close their garden gates. The Wards hang laundry between borrowed balconies, not because poverty is romantic, but because communal space is all they have.

**Night:** a listening terminal hums above the stairs. The quiet hours make a Collector's boots conspicuous. Street light is scarce and purposeful: amber at a counting device, a warm window over a family, dark machinery where an institution has stopped listening.

## 4. Political power and its checks

Meridian's Comptroller is elected for life by twelve senior members of the Board of Actuaries. The Ledger Assembly is representative in form but weights a member's vote by the registered allocation of their constituents. The unfilled Wards seat demonstrates exclusion without requiring a speech about it. A person with a high Omen can benefit from the system and still oppose it; neither wealth nor poverty determines every character's allegiance.

The Comptroller controls the Dawn Tally, transfer licenses and regional monopoly channels. The Board controls appointments, reserve appropriations and succession. Assembly committees issue budgets and public hearings, but enforcement depends on the same records that exclude the Wards. Three independent-looking checks therefore share one vulnerable accounting format. Ivo's corrected copies matter because a change in the record can reveal that dependency.

| Institution | Real leverage | Public justification | Internal split | What changes when challenged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Comptrollership | Allocations, licenses, protected routes | Stability prevents another catastrophic storm | Emergency protection versus permanent control | The final choice redistributes authority, not merely the title of ruler |
| Board of Actuaries | Appointments, reserve budgets, succession | Technical competence should govern arithmetic | Honest measurement versus institutional survival | Records can constrain the office if people can audit them |
| Ledger Assembly | Hearings, trade and civic budgets | Every registered constituency has a voice | High-Omen districts versus empty/underweighted seats | An accessible count and occupied Wards seat become concrete reform images |
| Local councils | Water, housing, food, festivals and roads | Residents know local needs | Local autonomy versus accepting protected allocations | Breaking a Seal requires emergency coordination, not instant utopia |
| Seal-holder offices | Regional monopoly on a control channel | Protection from the Deficit and promised permanence | Personal fear versus responsibility to residents | A freed channel returns agency, while original ledger artifacts remain in the Basilica archive until the finale |
| Courts and wardens | Witness records, disputes, safe passage | A true account must outlast rulers | Procedure versus evidence of a wrong count | A correction protects a person and a counter-copy rather than erasing history |

The TR 999 pacts renew old control; they did not build the TR 702 Basilica. Varro inherited his office and Seal in TR 1001. Thale inherited the system and chose Fixed Fate after discovering its hidden prisoner. His protected-route policies have real short-term benefits and identified hidden debits. The argument is who pays, who consents, and whether safety justifies abolishing everybody's future.

### The regional political map

Hyakki combines yokai courts with human village councils; Tamamo fears disappearance, while residents disagree about change. The River Empire's Chancellor dominates the surface economy, but the Ten Courts keep a separate account of the dead. Śūnyapura's scholar/guild council controls observatory access and water maintenance; Basilica surveyors exploit those permits. Kroton's Synod claims a numerical qualification to civic belonging; Kallisto organizes the people it excludes. Lucky Strike's sheriff has public law but the House has credit, property and the loop's contract. None of these governments is an uncomplicated good alternative to Meridian.

### Three storefront tests

The player should identify what a shop does from its tool and working activity before reading a sign. Its stock should have an origin and a reason to be low or abundant. Its keeper must have one opinion unrelated to the land's main superstition. A clinic worker who hates rain, a broker who keeps pigeons and a priest who cannot cook create inhabitants rather than cultural exhibits.

## 8. Infrastructure, homes and movement

**Food and water.** The capital depends on ordinary farms, delta barges and maintained canals. Luck can protect a shipment; it cannot create its contents. Water comes through elevated cisterns and gravity channels, making an upstream gate closure a political act as well as an environment prop. Śūnyapura's stepwells require cleaning and safe access, not merely mythic symbolism. The Crossroads loop can repeat a meal locally but cannot export infinite food; goods crossing its contract boundary reconcile against the source manifest.

**Energy and light.** Brass systems use stored mechanical work, fuel, wind/water drives and electrical contacts where the design establishes them. Nixie amber is a readable institutional accent, not magical floodlighting on every object. A luck vessel needs energy and maintenance as well as a valid claim. A broken mechanism may remain broken despite a favorable digit.

**Housing.** Plateau deeds and Wards occupancy records use different paperwork. That disparity explains who can repair a stair, claim damages or vote without making a high Omen necessary to breathe. Shared balconies, workrooms and kitchens respond to a shortage of safe space. Gardens require water, time and a protected site, so living green on the plateau is a visible class resource.

**Transport.** Lift fares can be discriminatory civic policy, not a scientific necessity. Public lifts have brakes, counterweights and inspection tags. Ferries connect Hyakki's islands; river junks move bulk goods through Bāfā; packet boats connect Kroton's coast; the monsoon road and Crossroads rail carry people between jurisdictions. Every crossing needs a manifest because the Basilica counts ownership and debt across borders.

**Communications.** Street criers, broadsheets, letters, mechanical boards and regionally established radio carry news. Screens remain limited to Basilica terminals and Exchange boards. A dead letter can preserve a witness; a price ticker can conceal a debt. Do not put modern phones or universal holographic interfaces into an ordinary home merely to explain a quest.

**Health and care.** Physical treatment, disability support, sanitation and collective protection still matter. A Civic 99 can be ill. A Civic 03 can be competent. The state can weaponize the cost of luck transfer, but the book must not endorse its claim that a vulnerable person is worth less. Livia's needs are not reduced to a number puzzle, and saving her does not excuse Varro's work.

**Education.** Naming introduces a child to a digit, not a destiny or career assignment. Schools teach arithmetic; Basilica textbooks turn arithmetic into moral doctrine. A teacher can teach the sum correctly and contest the conclusion. Children's clothes and proportions must remain age-appropriate; Hina's object age does not authorize adult sexualization of her childlike appearance.

## 9. Law, debt and the right to a copy

A lawful seizure needs a claimant, registered amount, source account, warrant authority, notice and appeal route. A Collector may carry a receipt without possessing a just cause. Civic fines and imprisonment can be abused even where the state claims not to execute people; forcing Civic settlement is a potentially lethal punishment and should be named as such, not described as humane arithmetic.

Audit courts can appoint a representative for someone who cannot stop a wheel. The duel tests a stated claim under agreed rules; it does not make the winner morally correct. A trial by a keeper is different again: objective seals, books or thread measure progress, not damage to a revered body. Keepers end satisfied and cannot be killed for a reward.

The physical counter-copy is a recurring political object. A poor citizen's receipt can resist a rewritten central ledger. Burning enforcement books without preserving witness records would erase the people Jinling wants to help. The story therefore distinguishes cancelling a debt from destroying evidence.

## 11. Story use and the manga camera

Use a prop before explaining it. First show a jar being secured, then a confiscation, then the source account that makes the theft legible. A large action panel establishes what moved and what was struck; the following close-up shows the meaningful fracture or released latch. Comedy follows comprehension: a clerk can file a heroic complaint in triplicate after the danger, but a joke must not erase Ama's fear or mock a real belief.

Recurring material can carry long buildup. NIL's interrupted ring first looks broken, later matches a missing path in an observatory diagram. Vesper's removed uniform insignia precedes her confession. Varro's exemption token is kept cleaner than his warrant sleeve. The finale reuses these objects as chosen actions rather than adding a new costume decoration to announce growth.

The manga and artbook share the same design IDs, fixed handedness and chronological outfits. A panel can simplify texture to keep movement readable, but cannot move the decoder to the other wrist. A generated number is never the source of truth for a game cue; exact Civic/Audit/resonance text is separately lettered from scene data. Page art, balloons and dialogue are exportable layers where the production pipeline permits it.

## 12. Acceptance and remaining production evidence

Every new scene asks: which account pays, what physical work remains, who may refuse, what evidence survives, and who experiences the consequence later? Every new power needs the domain contract in SYSTEMS rather than a larger light effect. Every storefront needs stock, a worker and a working tool. Every faction crowd must show both a shared construction language and individual lives.

This chapter is specified fictional world logic. It does not certify a market economy, legal system, mechanical simulation, translated script, native cultural review or player comprehension. The art catalog labels legacy blockouts and absent renders honestly; fresh concepts do not by themselves establish animation/rig readiness.
