# Existing Higgsfield asset inventory

8 November 2026. Selected local concept collection: **84 cast/outfit/encounter boards and seven regional environment boards**,91 unique selected images. A board is not necessarily a new person: outfits, shared group/uniform references and encounter archetypes have separate identities in the catalog. Source assets are under `art/concepts/complete/`; native generation/job records are in `art/catalog/higgsfield_artbook_outputs.json` and the production log. The new [94-page concept collection](../../output/pdf/ZERO-SUM_OMEN_Concept_Collection.pdf) assembles those selected images with scope and current-story guidance.

The collection's adjacent `.manifest.json` records every source path, actual SHA256, native job ID, board kind and page. Original PNGs remain unchanged; compressed JPEG print derivatives are embedded in the PDF. This collection did not submit a generation or spend Higgsfield credits. Its adjacent `.qa.json` records the PDF checks and visual-inspection scope.

## What the references provide

- Main cast and companions, Nil's outfit variants, secret recruits, bosses, keepers, story characters, faction uniforms and encounter archetypes have illustrated construction proposals.
- Regional boards cover Meridian, Hyakki, the River Empire, Śūnyapura, Kroton, Crossroads and Basilica, using top,2.5D and front compositions.
- Actual Blender scenes and four-camera evidence are a separate source class in the earlier complete artbook. These illustrations do not establish rigs, animation, topology, navigable levels or perfectly consistent props in every view.
- The Varro ally proposal retains the mature boss's body; Aka's corrected single-tail identity and the safe upright Vetala costume study are retained. The latter does not establish the hanging action pose.

## Remaining design coverage

Mara Vale and Wen were introduced in the resolved story revision after the selected concept pass; they have no dedicated character boards here. Baker has no dedicated selected board. Brother Nought has a character design but the shrine interior and its terminal do not yet have dedicated accepted construction plates. Worker archetypes do not supply every individual background face. Detailed scene geography, acting/expression sheets, costume continuity per scene and exact readable numeric lettering remain production work.

The old colour manga studies remain user-rejected. They are not included or repurposed as the new B&W story. Future story pages must use the authorized native Mangaka route, volume by volume, after prose and page-script review. This book contains late-story spoilers and is an editorial reference, not a spoiler-safe reader edition.
