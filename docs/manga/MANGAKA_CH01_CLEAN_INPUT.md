NIL(they/them,appears19) wants a name and an ordinary registered account. White hair with one contrasting streak, amber eyes, asymmetric dark coat with longer anatomical LEFT tail, three front brass clasps, fused broken-circle decoder on anatomical RIGHT wrist. They count when anxious; they are kind before they feel brave. NaN is their unresolved identity, not a name that suddenly becomes7. Their chosen resonance7 is a separate display. Ama Okoro is a working seamstress and Tobi Okoro her nine-year-old son. Brother Nought is an older, warm, tired shrine keeper. Collector is one procedural human official; Debt Hound is one articulated enforcement machine. Comptroller Lucan Thale is a controlled, mature adult Comptroller with a gear monocle and steel Civic50. Keep the cause of NIL’s strange identity unexplained.

01. Rain follows ordinary skilled workers through Meridian's Wards. NIL studies their Civic numbers, then their own NaN. Their immediate wish is to answer when someone asks their name.

02. Baker, a tired human breakfast seller, refuses a salvaged washer as money but offers work:three washed bowls for one bun. NIL anxiously counts. Embarrassment is gentle, not humiliation.

03. Tobi Okoro advises NIL to start with the clean bowls. NIL takes it literally, then catches the joke and almost smiles. Ama Okoro calls her son to help deliver a repaired coat.

04. Collector stops Ama Okoro under a narrow market arch. Establish canal rail, steps and exits. Ama Okoro's receipt disagrees with his authorization stamp; a nearby jar already holds six confiscated luck units.

05. Ama Okoro's Civic01 faces a pending final seizure. She requests a review; the official offers a deadline. Tobi Okoro grips the repaired coat. Let their relationship make the threat personal.

06. Baker warns NIL not to draw attention. NIL looks from the promised bun to the frightened family. Their hand shakes as they leave the unfinished washing to intervene anyway.

07. NIL asks where the jar's six units came from. Collector says this is official. "Then it should add up," NIL answers. The mechanism moves despite the unresolved discrepancy.

08. NIL tries an ordinary lever and fails. Brass scrapes their palm; their fused decoder burns. They see the open escape route and choose again to stay.

09. The interrupted decoder ring aligns with a missing return channel. Brief impressions of other people's helping hands disturb NIL. No explanatory voice tells them their origin.

10. NIL redirects a bounded transfer:the jar visibly loses six as Ama01 becomes07. Show both ends. Luck is rerouted, not created. NIL's own NaN remains.

11. Hold on Ama Okoro and Tobi Okoro's relief before danger resumes. Ama Okoro asks whom to thank. NIL cannot supply a name. She simply thanks them, without naming them herself. Baker gives the bun to Tobi Okoro, who presses it into NIL’s uninjured hand before the alarm; NIL pockets it.

12. Collector notices NaN and signals Debt Hound. Ama Okoro and Tobi Okoro escape toward the laundry stairs; NIL deliberately draws pursuit along the other route.

13. Re-establish arch, bridge and service stairs from above. Debt Hound has four articulated stamp feet. NIL's coat catches a laundry peg; one exasperated glance supplies a small physical joke after escape.

14. The machine seals an easy gate. NIL cannot open every channel on demand; the injured hand hurts. A resident blocks the lane with a laundry cart, buying seconds through ordinary courage.

15. NIL slips through a repaired door carved with an open hand. Move clearly from cold rain to a cramped warm shrine:lamp, ledgers, leaking-roof pan. Brother Nought hides the lamp from the search.

16. Brother Nought offers water,a cloth and a chair before asking for a story. NIL is confused by shelter without payment. Let their shoulders slowly loosen.

17. NIL offers the crushed bun as payment. Brother Nought divides it and takes the smaller piece. NIL nearly counts the fractions. Quiet warmth, not a joke at their expense.

18. NIL asks whether an unregistered person can owe anything. "They can try to bill you." Brother Nought then says a number does not make them a person; they already are one.

19. NIL admits wanting a name that stays,a place for an answer when asked who they are. Their speech is halting and embarrassed. Brother Nought listens instead of declaring them special.

20. Brother Nought offers rest and a back exit, or the Naming terminal if NIL chooses. It connects to city records; he cannot promise success. Shelter does not depend on accepting the Rite.

21. A child's old Naming ribbon teaches one distinction:a chosen resonance digit is not a person's Civic allocation. Avoid a lecture about every power system.

22. NIL tries to guess what answer Brother Nought wants. He puts away the options sheet. "I asked what you would choose." Let the silence turn anxiety into cautious hope.

23. NIL chooses7 for themself,not because prophecy calls it strongest. They deliberately place the RIGHT decoder wrist on the terminal. A normal five-fingered hand remains visible.

24. Brass detents settle. A separate resonance7 stabilizes. Brother Nought cautiously smiles; NIL gives a tiny unguarded smile. Hold this sincere hope before interrupting it.

25. The identity query still returns NaN. Keep7 and NaN in distinct frames. NIL waits for a process already finished. "Did I choose wrong?" "No," Brother Nought answers immediately.

26. A search mechanism approaches. Brother Nought reaches for the disconnect. NIL apologizes for breaking the machine. "Not you," Brother Nought says, angry at the system and protective of them.

27. The decoder pulses once. An old open-hand carving catches light; NIL feels watched without seeing who is watching or learning why. Brother Nought stops diagnosing and prepares an exit.

28. Connected relay boxes carry the alarm across the city. Ordinary workers react with different emotions. Exact public message:"REMAINDER DETECTED." It is an unexplained official label.

29. The Basilica's eye opens above the tiny shrine. NIL is frightened,not triumphant. Brother Nought gently takes the injured hand and opens the back door. Their hope of belonging has started a hunt.

30. High above the Wards,Comptroller Lucan Thale moves a cold dinner aside and reads the alert. Steel Civic50 does not spin. His tired quiet face says,"There you are." End on the stopped spoon beside the relay map.
