## Narrative gate

The series treatment defines an immediate personal want, a consequential first choice, an opening threat with a visible mechanism, changing relationships, distinct regional dramatic forms, and a clue/payoff ledger. Lore enters when a character needs it to decide something. A separate encyclopedia never substitutes for a scene.

Use a fixed authored resonance7 reference route with the Positive-Sum ending, labelled as an adaptation. Other game choices remain in game canon. Explain the four number domains through different actions, not one lecture. Reveal NIL's Remainder identity in chapters25–26 at the midpoint, then give them agency to respond; reveal Hap's confinement and Thale's final plan later. Do not hold every explanation until the final chapter.

## Drawing and emotion gate

Use original black ink, strong light/dark masses, selective screentone, clear anatomy and perspective, readable foreground/background separation. No grey conversion of the old colour pages. Faces must change with the event: guarded curiosity, embarrassment, fear, relief, anger, delight, shame and grief are distinct performances. Body posture, hands and silence support the expression. Simplified comedic reactions follow resolved dramatic beats; never make victims' suffering the joke.

One clear English left-to-right convention throughout; explicit scene transitions and establishing shots; no unexplained location/costume switch. A fight needs a goal, geography, rule/cost, failed approach, adaptation, decisive choice and aftermath. Repeating battle poses is not choreography. Dialogue sounds like the speaker and leaves room for acting. Avoid repeated exposition captions and a constant stoic squint.
