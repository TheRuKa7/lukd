## Chapter01 - No Account

**Promise:** a person seeking proof that they belong does something kind before they have any proof. **Emotional path:** curiosity -> embarrassment -> connection -> alarm -> frightened commitment -> relief -> hope -> rejection -> being hunted. **Question:** what happens to someone the world cannot count?

Follow NIL's boots through their reflections to an unreadable NaN. One brief caption only: "In Meridian, everyone has a number." **Page02.** NIL tries to buy breakfast with a salvaged washer, discovers it is not money, and offers work instead. The vendor is tired, not cruel. "Three bowls washed. One bun." **Page05.** NIL watches rather than announcing a mission. Ama asks for a review, gets a deadline. NIL looks from their own blank registration to Ama's readable but threatened01. Wanting an account suddenly feels different. They step into the arch anyway. **Page26.** A search mechanism descends toward the shrine window. Nought recognizes the terminal is requesting a category it cannot supply and reaches for the disconnect. **Page27.** The decoder and terminal resonate once. Other lamps in the shrine flutter; one old open-hand carving catches light. A voice-shaped breath seems to say "Pick" but no speaker is shown or named as a god. NIL feels watched. Nought stops trying to diagnose and starts preparing an exit.

**Page28.** Show the alarm spreading through physically connected relay boxes across Meridian. The ordinary workers from page1 stop mid-task. **Page29.** Full-page dominant image: the Basilica's eye opens over the small warm shrine. NIL is a frightened person at its base, not a triumphant chosen-one silhouette. Nought opens the back door and takes their injured hand gently. The chapter's personal hope has triggered a city-scale hunt.
