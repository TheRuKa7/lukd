## The story the reader is promised

A nameless young wanderer in a city that prices everyone by their luck tries to become an ordinary registered person. They risk their chance of belonging to help a mother. Their Naming registers a chosen7 but leaves their identity NaN, and wakes an institution that has been waiting for them. An apparently mercenary gunslinger stays. A feared collector has a sister he cannot save. The hunt crosses cultures whose different ways of understanding luck are being flattened into one ledger.

At the midpoint NIL discovers they are three centuries of discarded fractions made into one person. Knowing what they are does not answer what they are entitled to choose. The second half lets them react, set consent boundaries, change friendships and build an alternative to sacrifice. In the finale they confront a competent, grieving ruler who would abolish chance to prevent loss. They must open a machine that is also an institution, without deciding that everyone who relied on it can be abandoned.

The central question evolves: "Can I have a name?" -> "Who owns what I am?" -> "What can I choose to give?" The answer is earned through relationships and compatible records, not a final friendship beam that invents limitless power. This adaptation follows the Positive-Sum ending and resonance7/Horseshoe. Game alternatives remain game canon, not contradictory extra endings inside this linear story.

## Why this version will flow

Each scene begins because the previous choice changed someone's options. The prologue receipt leads to the Hyakki toll ledger; its creditor leads to the River Exchange; contradictory living/dead books lead to the observatory; suppression leads to the Sieve's accelerated policy; financing evidence and Vesper's history lead to the Crossroads; the recovered contract and six Seals lead back to the Basilica. Traveling is a decision with a reason and a cost. Every region contains work, food, repairs, humor and disagreement rather than a uniform population explaining a national gimmick.

The regional dramatic forms differ. Meridian is a rescue/hunt and reluctant partnership. Hyakki is a festival household story about permanence and being real. River is a financial/legal investigation with a family betrayal. Shunyapura is scientific discovery and an identity crisis during a monsoon. Kroton is a prisoner escort turning into a civic rescue. Crossroads is a repeat-night mystery about protective control. Basilica is a return home, institutional confrontation and deliberate ethical choice.

No encyclopedia scene. One new rule when it changes a person's action; show the rule's cost and a counter. A high number is neither virtue nor competence. Skilled low-number workers are ordinary and useful. Innocent suffering, poverty, grief, disability and cultural practices are never the punchline. Humor comes from institutional contradictions, precise observation, Vesper's generosity under bravado, Maeve's practical superstition, Hina's grand pronouncements and Prima's earnestness.

## Visual performance

Original inked adventure manga: confident variable-weight black contours, purposeful spotted blacks, selective crosshatching and screentone, readable perspective and physical contact. Draw characters acting inside a place, not posed portraits with speech placed above them. Establish geography before close combat. Preserve the line of action, anatomical handedness and held objects between panels. Show a wind-up, a path, contact, reaction and changed situation. Stop after a decisive hit long enough to feel its consequence.

Use distinct facial performances: NIL's guarded curiosity, anxious counting, embarrassment, hope, disappointment, fear, focused effort, relief and tentative joy. Vesper's practiced grin can slip into shame or concern; it is not permanently printed on her face. Nought's warmth has fatigue. Varro's eyes and posture act around the worn mask. The funny beat often uses a still face and a held silence; a child in peril does not get a gag close-up. Major revelation pages may be quiet rather than another spectacular splash.

In B&W, identity depends on silhouette, black/white costume masses, hair pattern, tools and expression. NIL: white hair with a single dark/screentone streak marking the orange lock in colour references, long black asymmetric coat, longer anatomical LEFT tail, fused broken-circle decoder on RIGHT wrist, three front abacus clasps. Vesper: dark triangular dealer coat and hat, LEFT braid, lip scar, two hat cards, RIGHT-hand seven-chamber gun. Varro: mature broad heavy body, rust coat represented by distinct tone, three glass jars, full strapped hound-skull mask until ActV. Preserve age, face and body through outfit changes.

A page normally has3-5 panels, but the scene controls it. Do not repeat the same grid for660 pages. A dialogue pair can use eyes/hands and negative space. A chapter turn should change a question or option, not simply stop halfway through a fight. Use small domestic observations after earned relief. Avoid tiny dense word balloons; dialogue should be short enough to leave room for the performance.

## Four number domains, introduced separately

1. Civic Omen00-99 is a registered allocation affecting risk/access, not morality. Ama01 becomes07 when six units visibly leave a confiscation jar. A Civic00 event is dangerous; a different combat meter reaching0 is not automatic death.
2. Resonance0-9 is a chosen practice digit. NIL chooses7 in Chapter01; it does not repair NaN. Choosing0 is not civic death and must not be mocked as a bad choice.
3. Balance is a declared temporary Audit stake. It is not fen money and a victory does not automatically confiscate an opponent's life or mint civic luck.
4. Audit Omen99-00 is the normalized readout of that stake in a separate frame. NIL's NaN identity remains visible beside it. Varro survives his first loss with Civic61 unchanged.

Every luck transfer names a source, amount and destination. Ordinary mechanics consume fuel, ammunition, paper, labour and maintenance; magic does not replace engineering. Powers expose permitted possibilities and stop bounded outcomes through instruments/contracts. NIL cannot teleport, clone themself, stop any event without a channel, revive anyone for free or invent a new verb whenever the plot needs one.

The six schools are distinct practices: Ledger Auditing names claims and channels; Shrine Binding uses kept objects and vows; Brush Writing preserves records while amending them; Karma Weaving banks declared deeds/returns; Sieve Geometry applies number relations with exclusions; Dealing trades informed conditions through explicit contracts. NIL learns Splice/Carry/Rewrite/Place Value/Factor/Deal at the appropriate encounters. In the story a physical tool, a permission, a limit and an aftermath make each power legible.
