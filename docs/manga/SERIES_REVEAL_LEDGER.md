## Reader-knowledge rules

Every revelation needs an observable cause, a person affected and a later decision changed by knowing it. Label an unproved idea as a character’s hypothesis. An omniscient caption cannot quietly certify a mistaken doctrine. A secret withheld in dialogue is also withheld in artwork, flashbacks, chapter previews and image briefs: no early open cage, omniscient construction scene or explanatory character caption.

The reader may suspect NIL’s origin before the midpoint; the story must not prove it early. End chapter11, page330, with independent evidence converging on NIL. Chapter12 opens with the unambiguous Remainder conclusion and gives the emotional response time before another fight. Consent to an examination never means consent to publication, experimentation without limit or public ownership of NIL.
