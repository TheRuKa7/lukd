## Volume 1 — A Name on Loan: Meridian

1. **Three Bowls** — An unregistered wanderer seeks a repair apprenticeship and washes bowls for breakfast. Tobi's joke offers connection; Ama's contested seizure forces the wanderer to step into view and interrupt the Collector before the chapter ends.
2. **The Jar Owes Six** — NIL risks exposure to transfer six confiscated units back to Ama. The source and recipient are visible, and the Debt Hound gives chase.
3. **A Door That Opens** — A stranger impedes the pursuit. Nought gives NIL food and shelter before asking anything, then offers a voluntary Naming.
4. **Seven Is Not a Name** — The wanderer adopts Nil as a personal name before choosing resonance 7. The name is accepted between people, but the institutional identity result remains NaN. The alarm reaches Thale as hope turns into fear.
5. **Seven Fen** — Vesper helps first and disguises concern as a fee. A solo Audit teaches NIL the difference between a temporary stake and Civic standing.
6. **A Window at Six** — Vesper and NIL cooperate, Gus repairs a strap and offers a free charm, and Livia's guarded window gives the chase a human cost.
7. **A Receipt for Mercy** — Varro's jar creates a legible tactical problem. NIL spares him; Maeve's gift, Seal 1 and a toll receipt make a journey to Hyakki possible. Mercy costs time and does not excuse Varro's actions.

## Volume 2 — The Unfinished Hundred: Hyakki

8. **The Ferryman's Seam** — Tetsu carries fugitives across the water; the old Collector seam on his coat hints that people can leave institutions.
9. **A Hundred Wishes** — Hina shows NIL her ordinary wishes and the Night Parade; a neighbour fades before either can make the danger abstract.
10. **The Keeper's Gift** — Elderly human shrinekeeper Ofuku's care for Hina establishes a history of affection, while Tamamo promises permanence.
11. **Paper Without a Name** — Enma's nonlethal trial leads to a ledger with no page for NIL. Its absence raises a question and does not establish an origin.
12. **The Foxes' Bargain** — Ao and Aka obstruct the path for different reasons. Hina is tempted by a promise that answers her deepest fear.
13. **Already Real** — Hina refuses permanence purchased with NIL's freedom. Tamamo survives defeat, and Hina claims personhood at 99.
14. **The Toll Changes Hands** — Hina protects Enma's evidence; Seal 9 and a released toll ledger transfer an obligation to the River Exchange.

## Volume 3 — The Price of Shelter: River Empire

15. **Purchased Guests** — Jinling buys the bounty and offers refuge with terms the group can question. NIL notices that hospitality and control can look alike.
16. **Tea and Interest** — Jinling's wit gives way to evidence of secret debt relief. Vesper recognizes a mark connected to a hand she once rigged.
17. **Four Lanterns** — Bai Si counts his lanterns and recalls an individual omitted by the institution he serves.
18. **The Weight of the Dead** — Paired wardens test cooperation; living accounts and independently preserved death records disagree in the same direction.
19. **The Brush Trial** — Judge Cui offers a bounded correction. Bai Si joins because correction is possible, not because centuries of grief disappear.
20. **The True Account** — Jinling confronts her father and protects the true book. Vesper admits the child harmed by her rigged hand.
21. **A Debt to the Living** — Public reform is harder than private relief. The party secures Seal 8; protected records and an unpaid release order lead it toward independent measurement.

## Volume 4 — What the Numbers Cannot Own: Śūnyapura

22. **Room for Chai** — Shunya's hospitality creates space for NIL to set limits on investigation. Meera's loss makes the missing record personal.
23. **The Drowned Record** — The party recovers Meera's brother's account without pretending the record can resurrect him.
24. **Terms of Examination** — Shunya explains an examination's scope and uncertainty; NIL can agree, stop or refuse publication.
25. **Everyone's Handwriting** — Independent signatures and fractional discrepancies converge on NIL. Companions react as people affected, not a chorus of exposition.
26. **But You Are Yours** — NIL learns they emerged from accumulated discarded fractions. They remain a distinct person and choose what to ask next.
27. **Brightness: Varies** — Shunya offers conditional co-authorship. The group confronts the Basilica's invented eclipse machines and secures Seal 0.
28. **An Order Accelerated** — Thale responds by accelerating the Sieve. The equation and its consent boundaries travel with NIL as evidence, not a weapon.

## Volume 5 — Catch the Falling: Kroton

29. **The Even Prime** — Prima escorts the party, sincere in a doctrine whose exceptions already trouble her.
30. **The City Beneath** — Kallisto's workers reveal who maintains the shining terraces. An act of ordinary rescue challenges Prima's assumptions.
31. **The School Above the Sieve** — A school is scheduled to fall; Prima disobeys her teacher and accepts help to hold the children.
32. **A Thread That Measures** — The Moirai test sequence, cooperation and informed sacrifice. Relationships can be measured without ranking human worth.
33. **The First Gear** — Maeve finds machinery older than Thale's tenure; Bai Si remembers one person rather than an abstract total.
34. **What We Refuse to Cut** — The group protects its evidence and adapts when the Archon removes declared faces. No companion is sacrificed as a mandatory proof.
35. **Catch the Falling** — Prima catches her defeated teacher with help. Maeve forges the gear-horseshoe; the party secures Seal 2, and a financing trail points to Crossroads.

## Volume 6 — The Night That Knows: Crossroads

36. **An Honest Deal** — The Stranger teaches the limits of a fair hand. Vesper tells the group enough of her history for them to choose the risk.
37. **The Motel at Three** — Fibi knows routines inside a repeating night, but NIL says something she cannot predict.
38. **One Thousand Five Hundred Ninety-Six** — Penny and Vance corroborate the completed loops. Fibi lets the visitors see the cost of her apparent protection.
39. **The Price of Protection** — A contract's maintenance drain causes Fibi's fatal dawn. Her informed choice, not another person's promise, controls the response.
40. **The Marked Deck** — Vesper plays honestly and accepts loss while the group obtains the cancellation terms.
41. **A First New Sunrise** — Fibi and the House enact the clause; Fibi survives. The black-book page, marked deck and Seal 7 become protected evidence.
42. **The Letter from Meridian** — Livia's transfer becomes possible; Varro removes his mask and offers help as a guest. The way back to the Basilica opens at a cost.

## Volume 7 — An Open Hand: Basilica

43. **Eight Things Kept** — At camp, the group checks its distinct proofs and relationships; NIL hears the risks and chooses collective translation.
44. **The Seventh Socket** — Six office Seals and NIL's chosen 7 occupy separate sockets. Ivo pays off his earlier corrections by opening the service route.
45. **The Closed Fist** — Original ledgers and the physical cage reveal a starving Hap. Earlier dreams were incomplete, not an omniscient account of the truth.
46. **The Inherited Box** — Evidence distinguishes the TR702 cage, Thale's inheritance of office in TR985 and his discovery of Hap in TR999.
47. **Fixed Fate** — Thale's grief explains his fear but does not excuse informed coercion. The group exposes the hidden payers and faces imposed stillness.
48. **Hands That Answer** — Specific acts of help answer NIL's apparent isolation. Thale survives, asks what the others would do, and must face responsibility.
49. **An Open Hand** — Eight consenting contributors translate their evidence together. Hap is freed, NIL remains a person with a separate Civic 07 and resonance 7, and the epilogue shows repair as continuing work; a year later Hina turns 100.
