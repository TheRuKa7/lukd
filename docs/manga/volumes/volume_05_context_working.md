## Luck, population and the Remainder

Luck biases physically possible uncertain outcomes within an active channel. It cannot make an impossible outcome possible, invent knowledge, reverse completed death or eliminate the need for materials and labour. The Civic allocation is a standing share of access to the system's protection, not a literal percentage chance of surviving each day. There is no universal conversion from a Civic point to probability. A specified event's alternatives, hazards, competing claims and channel determine the bias. A larger allocation gives more leverage among comparable claims but never guarantees success.

Repeated risky events exhaust a channel's contracted reserve and may require a documented replenishment. An arbitrary happy coincidence is not evidence of an invisible new power. The author must be able to identify whether an outcome arose from skill, physical cause, a declared channel or ordinary chance. Readers need the decisive distinction in a scene, not an actuarial equation before every action.

Externalities are part of a channel's claim. A road contract cannot create safety by invisibly passing a crash to a named innocent bystander; any such transfer is an illicit redirection with a traceable debit. Thale's protected districts divert reserve access and maintenance budgets from poorer districts, increasing their exposure to ordinary hazards. The narrative does not depict every fortunate event as a magical assault on a stranger.

The conserved pre-finale total includes registered allocations, public and private reserves, unallocated capacity and the hidden Remainder. Birth establishes a dependent allocation drawn from a household or district pool; the Naming at seven records a chosen practice digit, not a first soul or first entitlement to safety. Death releases a registered share through witnessed probate; dependents have a legally defined claim before the balance returns to the pool. Fraud and exclusion can obstruct that process. No birth creates luck; no death deletes it. Animal and ecosystem channels are registered collectively in watershed and habitat reserves, not as numbered human citizens. Omens do not certify that only registered human life matters.

People without paperwork still exist and retain dependent or unclaimed ordinary allocations. Their missing documents do not produce Nil's NaN. Nil alone is an unlisted self-aware account for which the merged Ledger has no ordinary identity entry. The state can misdescribe many undocumented people without making them supernatural anomalies.

The Basilica routes Hap-mediated possibilities through regional accounts. Each Dawn Tally truncates incompatible fractional claims into an unlisted holding account. Over three centuries this accumulation becomes Nil. The fractions carry traces of many lives; the underlying possibility passes through captive Hap. Nil is therefore neither Hap reincarnated nor a detached child of a god. The midpoint establishes the many-life accumulation; the finale reveals the captive source and extraction process. Divine connection confers no moral rank, unique right to rule or ownership by contributors.

Equalizing displayed allocations would not repair broken infrastructure or release captured channels. More importantly, hoarders and protected institutions control the reserves and restrict lawful transfers. The central scarcity is worsened by policy, monopoly and inaccessible Remainder capacity. The novel must not portray destitution as a natural moral necessity of mathematics.

## Political world and regional consequences

The Basilica originally won support by coordinating storm protection, trade and disaster reserves. Its present supporters include patients, transport workers and households whose predictable coverage is real. Its opposition includes municipal reformers, local keepers, reserve mutuals and separatists who disagree about how much common coordination to retain. Neither group is culturally or morally uniform.

Regional Seals are monopoly credentials. Breaking one reopens a locally accountable channel; it does not crown the winner, transfer a culture's ownership to Nil or magically settle public policy. Original records remain in the Basilica until the return. Keepers can deny access on procedural grounds and be persuaded by evidence or a bounded trial; reverence is not obtained through bodily defeat.

Hyakki's dispute divides restoration workers who need stable vows, younger spirits who want permission to change and a court terrified of further fading. In the River Empire, dock cooperatives and small lenders dispute Jinling's selective relief as well as her father's predatory claims. At the observatory, instrument crews require wages and safe weather access while scholars debate publication. Kroton has Synod adherents who maintain real shelters alongside residents excluded by its ranking. Crossroads employees rely on House wages while organizing against exclusions. These positions recur through letters, representatives and consequences after the party departs.

Jinling cancels debts under eight fen from the Exchange owner's distributable surplus, not depositor funds or workers' wages. She transfers reserve oversight to a provisional board including workers, depositors and municipal clerks. Her protected ownership stake and reputation remain contested. Relief is a funded first action, not an instant cure of class power.

Thale considered decentralized emergency allocation after discovering Hap in TR999 and rejected a Board proposal because it would dilute executive control and might fail during a storm. His concealment is a deliberate choice, not lack of an available alternative. Ivo retains the rejected proposal; the party obtains it alongside dated release records. Thale's argument remains serious because transition risk is real, but he cannot claim no other course was offered.

## The finale's mechanism and legitimacy

Positive-Sum ends the Basilica's compulsory single-format rounding and monopoly on channels. It does not promise an infinite reservoir, perfect safety, equal outcomes or the abolition of uncertainty. The equation demonstrates bounded translation between independently meaningful records, allowing possibilities the old normalization discarded to circulate without erasing their differences. Freed Hap participates but cannot command the new institutions or unilaterally seize allocations.

The final eight contributors authorize use of their protected records, not disposal of their persons or national sovereignty. Before the final journey, regional provisional councils and reserve stewards grant witnessed, revocable mandates for a bounded transition. The mandate is contested and limited; the companions do not claim universal assent. Essential local coverage continues from named emergency pools while the central channel switches over. Maeve's fitting holds the route open; Ivo's rejected decentralization proposal supplies an institutional starting point; the proofs make translation testable.

The first field test in Śūnyapura fails to preserve an event's timing despite balancing quantities. The Kroton test succeeds for a small consenting rescue team but cannot scale through a still-locked monopoly channel. By the finale the reader has seen a failure, a bounded success, a remaining barrier and the cost of transition. The final mechanism pays off this work rather than inventing a new spell.

Post-finale oversight belongs to regional reserve councils with elected resident representatives, technical stewards and independent witness records. Emergency seizures require review and expire; permanent forced Civic settlement is prohibited. A joint appeals panel compares counter-copies in public. People may refuse private contracts; essential civic protection cannot depend on purchasing a personal oath. Nil holds no hereditary office, remains a repair apprentice by choice and can refuse further examination. Thale relinquishes executive control, preserves records and faces public proceedings; tending pigeons is an image of diminished power, not acquittal. Some injuries and deaths remain irreparable. Hina's birthday occurs a year later amid continuing work.

## Meridian: a useful system that can refuse a person

Independent-looking authority is therefore vulnerable to the same excluded entry. This is a specific institutional dependency, not proof that every clerk is wicked.

Ward mechanics, clinicians and households organize mutual aid but cannot freely draw from another district's reserve. A lawful seizure requires a named claim, amount, source, warrant and appeal route. The appeal is formally real but can be inaccessible in time to a person facing Civic00. Ama's case shows that gap; her skill and testimony exist before the protagonist helps. Baker's stock, the resident's cart and Nought's limited rooms make assistance costly and finite.

The opening's three relationships demonstrate different kinds of power: a registrar can deny a credential; an employer can refuse sponsorship; a Collector can enforce a contested debit.
