# Character development dossiers

Authored Phase3 trajectories for the current49-chapter edition. Read with [societies and relationships](SOCIETIES_AND_RELATIONSHIPS.md) and [the scene ledger](SCENE_LEDGER.md). These contain future knowledge for authors, not a command to reveal it in an early chapter. Scheduled anchors are selected decisive moments, not the complete appearance list. Names label existing identities; group and indirect-reference coverage is recorded separately.

## Nil

Actor type: person_or_supernatural_person. Decisive chapters: 1,4,16,17,26,43,49.

**Want:** A repair apprenticeship and an unrenegotiated bed.

**Fear:** That uncertainty makes them disposable.

**Mistaken belief:** Being the correct useful answer will secure belonging.

**Competence:** Notices channels, mechanical faults and precise inconsistencies.

**Harm or cost:** Overstates a tentative comparison and freezes a cooperative reserve; must correct it publicly.

**Independent relationship:** Vesper's concealment makes trust costly; Maeve learns to ask instead of fixing them.

**Voice:** Short concrete questions; silently counts under stress; becomes more direct when refusing; never a walking equation glossary.

**Private life:** Keeps usable screws and likes the first clean click of a repaired mechanism.

**End state:** Chooses paid apprenticeship rather than office, retains name and bodily autonomy, accepts limits to what repair can return.

## Vesper

Actor type: person_or_supernatural_person. Decisive chapters: 5,20,35,40,41.

**Want:** Keep the new group alive without letting it examine her past.

**Fear:** That honesty will end the relationships she relies on.

**Mistaken belief:** Protection and charm can substitute for accountability.

**Competence:** Reads tells, plans exits, handles an actual loaded seven-chamber weapon.

**Harm or cost:** Her rigged hand harmed Mara; disclosure may cost companionship and restitution cannot purchase forgiveness.

**Independent relationship:** Fibi asks her to leave; Mara refuses reunion; she must obey both rather than perform redemption.

**Voice:** Drawling wagers and deflections; jokes stop when a victim names a fact; plain verbs in testimony.

**Private life:** Cleans the cylinder after rain and keeps losing at games she insists were practice.

**End state:** Gives testimony and restitution, accepts a fair loss, remains answerable and is not universally forgiven.

## Maeve

Actor type: person_or_supernatural_person. Decisive chapters: 6,7,10,33,35.

**Want:** Make her family's failed craft useful again.

**Fear:** That asking reveals she cannot solve everything.

**Mistaken belief:** A technically excellent repair is necessarily a kindness.

**Competence:** Forge tolerances, load-bearing fittings and patient practical instruction.

**Harm or cost:** Removes Hina's valued patch without permission and must undo her own competent work.

**Independent relationship:** Hina contradicts her repair instinct; she learns to work under another person's brief.

**Voice:** Concrete material verbs, occasional three-part lists; exasperation is physical rather than a continuous joke.

**Private life:** Saves failed test pieces and labels why they failed instead of selling them.

**End state:** Forges the requested fitting, keeps the failed pieces, helps teach rather than claiming Nil as a creation.
