# Phase2 continuity decisions and schedules

This is the planning handoff for the49-chapter edition, read with [the scene ledger](SCENE_LEDGER.md) and [resolved bible](RESOLVED_SERIES_BIBLE.md). It is not finished prose or a22-page-per-chapter script. No chapter or image generation is authorized by a structural PASS alone.

## Clock and geography

Relative Day1 is the Meridian opening in TR1002. Days1–2 cover the hunt and drain-gate aftermath; Day3 crosses by paid ferry; Days4–7 cover Hyakki; Days8–13 River; Days14–20 the observatory journey and investigation; Days21–26 Kroton; Day27 the westbound road; Day28 night the active Crossroads loop; Day29 the new dawn; Day30 the return convoy leaves. Three travel days take the party to the Meridian camp on Day33. The Basilica confrontation occurs Day34, transition and early aftermath Days35–36. The birthday epilogue explicitly jumps one year. Relative dates do not invent an unestablished month length or replace the TR history.

All regional handoffs consume travel time and paid provisions. Regional letters use the courier/ferry/caravan routes established on departure; replies to the Day20 request arrive by Day30 and are examined Day33. Ivo's archive copies first reach the party in chapter21 through the Exchange correspondence route; Ivo does not suddenly appear at a distant camp. Livia's independent medical transfer uses Nought/Gus's Meridian route, not the party's westbound convoy. Her consent and care confirmation arrive by letter before Varro accompanies the return.

**Loop clarification superseding the ambiguous older phrasing:** The House commissions the protection contract in TR999. Its fatal protective loop first activates on Day28 of the TR1002 story when its dawn-threat trigger fires; it did not imprison an entire region for three outside years. Fibi is18 during the main story; the protection was arranged when she was15. Before the party enters,1,596 iterations have completed within the field; the party enters iteration1,597. Chapters37–40 occupy that same iteration and chapter41 prevents its fatal reset. The novel shows corroborated prior repetitions rather than deliberately killing Fibi again as a diagnostic. Outside the field that night advances once. This preserves the established counter without converting subjective iterations into TR years.

Fibi retains memory under the named beneficiary clause; Penny and Vance retain only the expressly authorized token/case records. The arriving party retains knowledge through Nil's unregistered-account anchor. The penny tokens are nonspendable counters, not newly minted fen. Food, equipment and ordinary winnings do not accumulate across resets. The House's combat rewind is a bounded Audit mechanic, not a power to undo an already completed public cancellation at will.

## Seals, permissions and prior tests

Power progression is Splice's accidental bounded return in2, supervised basic Audit use5–6, Carry11, Rewrite19, Place Value26, Factor32 and Deal36. No chapter may use a later verb merely because its source art or game specification already exists. Place Value is introduced on an inert instrument on the following voluntary evening after Nil's refused afternoon examination, not by treating consent as permanent. The rescue-team translation31 concerns a limited agreed account channel; it is not early Factor or unlimited creation. Failed or refused personal examinations do not revoke access to ordinary instruction.

Seals1/9/8/0/2/7 are released in chapters7/14/21/27/35/41 respectively. Chapter44 uses those six office credentials plus chosen resonance7 in a separate seventh socket. Equal glyphs do not merge keyed sockets. Recalled guards, ritual lock and Ivo's internal passage remain separate obstacles.

Hyakki's restoration agreement14 names local stewards and a return correspondence address. River's public board21 records a limited mandate to investigate, not carte blanche to seize reserves. Vidya's measured reports28 invite all affected stewards to consider a bounded transition. Kroton residents35 appoint their own route witnesses. Crossroads staff41 begin their own property/wage proceedings. Those networks return conditional replies42; delegates ratify a specific, revocable transition43 after recording opposition and the limits of representation. Councils' essential coverage pools stay separate from private wagers.

The test in23 fails safely on an inert instrument: equal quantities deliver at different intervals. The28 model corrects that timing for a defined class of claims and records uncertainty. In31 a small consenting rescue team demonstrates that bounded class with named source/target stakes. It cannot scale through a locked central control channel; the trial does not create an unlimited pre-finale attack. Chapters43/48 schedule the pools and withdrawal conditions before49 uses the unlocked route.

## Dreams and residual obligations

Dreams follow chapters7/14/21/28/35/42: fortune-teller/Pick; paper lantern/Real; money toad/Owed; monsoon/Change; cracked statue/Worth; slot machine/Tomorrow. Dice eyes and orbiting digits recur; faces vary. The final dream may suggest the eye's inward viewpoint, but has no bars, fist, captive body or confirmed physical map. Nil's prior chosen resonance is repeated, never reset. Regional dialogue introduces the outlawed Hap tradition21 before interpreting later dreams.

The conclusion preserves necessary losses: Wen is dead, Mara is not obliged to forgive, illness still needs medicine, some spirits still need care, and councils still disagree. Thale's public proceedings are a consequence of preserved evidence. Nil's apprenticeship pays off chapter1's ordinary desire without making them ruler. Hina's birthday is explicitly after the year jump. These obligations are carried into chapter49, not deferred to a required sequel.
