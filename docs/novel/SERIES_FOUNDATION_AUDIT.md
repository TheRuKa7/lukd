## Story understood so far

In Meridian, NIL wants a name and ordinary belonging. Their impossible NaN identity draws institutional attention after they help return six confiscated units to Ama. The encounter with Vesper, Varro's coercive debt and Nought's shelter introduce conflicting ideas of duty, debt and protection. A toll receipt gives the group a reason to go to Hyakki rather than travel at random.

Each region presents a concrete challenge to a different idea of worth: Hyakki's fading spirits and Hina's fear of unreality; the River Exchange's bought debts and conflicting living/dead records; Śūnyapura's investigation and the question of consent; Kroton's Sieve and Prima's choice to save children; Crossroads' repeating night and Fibi's allegedly protective contract; and the Basilica's inherited machinery and Thale's coercive Fixed Fate. These conflicts must change the people involved and send the group to the next region for a legible reason.

At the intended midpoint, independent evidence establishes that NIL emerged from accumulated discarded fractions. NIL remains a distinct person with agency over examination and publication. At the finale, the party discovers the physical confinement of Hap, confronts Thale's informed choices and uses distinct records with consent to support a different way of sharing possibility. Ordinary pre-finale powers do not create unlimited Civic luck. The conclusion must show who performs the practical work of change, not only announce a new principle.

## Rules the story cannot silently change

- Civic Omen 00–99, chosen resonance 0–9, Audit Balance and normalized Audit Omen are different things. NIL's NaN is not replaced by choosing 7. Ordinary Audit defeat does not automatically kill a person or set Civic to 00.
- Before the final change of regime, an observable luck transfer has an identified source and destination. Ama's 01→07 is funded by six units leaving the jar. A working mechanism needs a channel, permissions and limits.
- Six office Seals are acquired in order 1, 9, 8, 0, 2, 7; NIL's chosen resonance occupies the separate seventh socket. Guard recall, lock and service route are separate obstacles.
- The cage predates Thale: the recorded chronology distinguishes TR702 construction, TR985 inheritance and TR999 discovery. Maeve's grandmother experienced the TR990 tightening, not original construction. These dates still need a chapter-by-chapter evidence plan.
- The eight protected proofs are Enma's page, true accounts, dead count, remainder equation, Lachesis's thread, first-gear horseshoe, marked deck and Black Book page. Map acquisition, custody, copies and final use before prose is accepted.
- Hina remains 99 until the epilogue; Bai Si has four physical lanterns; Ofuku is an elderly human shrinekeeper. Rahu/Ketu combat machines are invented constructs, not the revered celestial figures.

## Conflicts and gaps already found

1. **NIL's origin needs a precise causal formulation.** Correction after closer reading: WORLD_BIBLE §2A and §3 explicitly connect the registered allocations and discarded fractions to captive Hap, intending a two-stage reveal. It was premature to call the two descriptions automatically contradictory. Reconcile the allocation model in one causal account and distinguish midpoint evidence from finale knowledge; assess whether divine origin inadvertently makes NIL's worth instrumental. See [the literary critique](LITERARY_CRITIQUE.md).
2. **The seven-volume outline remains a scene inventory.** It has 49 chapter labels but no complete scene sequence, causal reversals, chapter-length rhythm, point-of-view plan or reviewed endings. It cannot support an assurance of polished pacing yet.
3. **Companion arcs are scheduled in older chapter numbers.** The four-stage relationship ledger and eight proofs need a complete mapping to chapters 1–49, including what happens between recruitment and the finale. The 22-chapter ledger is an input, not the new schedule.
4. **World operations need story consequences.** Economy, shops, work, institutions and powers are specified in reference documents, but the seven-volume outline does not yet show a continuous political and economic response to the party's actions or practical post-finale transition.
5. **The existing opening is provisional.** The first three prose chapters were drafted before this full-series analysis. Their hook, chapter scale, characterization, location logic, name/identity treatment, power limits and alignment with the eventual ending need an editorial pass after the series foundation is settled.
6. **Context and production targets remain open.** Seven working context dossiers exist, but none meets the requested 6,000 substantive lines. The full prose novel, 49 page-by-page manga adaptations and counted exports do not yet exist. A previous large Mangaka manuscript failed analysis; clean volume-specific upload derivatives require separate testing.
