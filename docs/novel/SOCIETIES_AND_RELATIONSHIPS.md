# Phase3 societies, relationships and voice

Current story-development specification for the49-chapter edition. Read with [the resolved bible](RESOLVED_SERIES_BIBLE.md), [character trajectories](CHARACTER_DOSSIERS.md) and [scene ledger](SCENE_LEDGER.md). This supplies decisions and scene obligations, not finished dialogue, a cultural endorsement or a simulation of every institution. Full-series spoilers are editorial context and must be removed from early-volume upload derivatives.

## Meridian: a useful system that can refuse a person

The Basilica coordinates channels, reserve budgets and emergency transport. Its real constituents include patients, market traders and residents who remember severe storms. The Comptroller controls execution; the Board controls appropriations and succession; an Assembly can hold hearings, but all three depend on the same accounting format. Independent-looking authority is therefore vulnerable to the same excluded entry. This is a specific institutional dependency, not proof that every clerk is wicked.

Ward mechanics, clinicians and households organize mutual aid but cannot freely draw from another district's reserve. A lawful seizure requires a named claim, amount, source, warrant and appeal route. The appeal is formally real but can be inaccessible in time to a person facing Civic00. Ama's case shows that gap; her skill and testimony exist before the protagonist helps. Baker's stock, the resident's cart and Nought's limited rooms make assistance costly and finite.

The opening's three relationships demonstrate different kinds of power: a registrar can deny a credential; an employer can refuse sponsorship; a Collector can enforce a contested debit. Do not give all three identical circular jokes. A licensed repair foreman can later sponsor Nil without diagnosing their cosmology. At the end Nil chooses a paid trial with transparent terms, not an unconditional lifetime reward from a grateful city.

Ivo's copies expose a rejected alternative in21; their existence does not make him innocent of earlier administration. Nought's Open Hand network supplies seats, transport contacts and witness copies, not a secret all-powerful replacement government. Nought does not know the cage's location before45. Gus stays within established dispatch routes and cannot personally attend each regional crisis. Livia's treatment remains ordinary medicine plus separately funded travel protection.

On departure7 the Wards still face quotas and damaged equipment. Letters21 and42 make those consequences visible. At return43 delegates can challenge Nil's transition risk; by49 an interim reserve service runs under witnesses, while former officials face review. Ama's receipt and the cart resident's damage claim remain legitimate business after the heroic event.

## Hyakki: preservation, recognition and permission to change

Human village councils, the Yokai Court, shrine workers and manifestation-dependent spirits negotiate access to local vows. Tamamo's court supplies actual shelter; its permanence bargain is attractive because losses are real. Restoration workers fear abrupt closure of stable channels, younger spirits fear never being allowed to change, and humans disagree over who funds care. No one speech speaks for the islands.

Hina is a person at99. The hundred-year expectation concerns court recognition and stable manifestation, not moral worth or the moment a soul becomes entitled to care. Ofuku's preference for preserving a familiar form can clash with Hina's wish to travel. Maeve's patch removal makes that disagreement physical and allows Hina to defend a preference without performing a grand metaphysical argument.

Ao values continuity and Aka questions the promise's conditions; both fear losing dependents. Neither twin is a mood-colored duplicate. Their paired speech is a practiced court form, not a biological inability to disagree. Each has one tail and retains the approved anatomical distinction. Tamamo's defeat ends a monopoly claim, not the existence of the court or a whole culture's truth.

The14 agreement names human and spirit care stewards, a queue of funded repairs and an address served by Tetsu. Ofuku retains a counter-copy of Enma's page. The reply42 permits only a bounded transition with care coverage preserved. During the epilogue some spirits still need restoration; Hina's birthday celebrates an actual future, not a cure imposed on every object.

## River Empire: relief without ownership of the relieved

The Exchange controls credit, collateral and reserve transaction timing. The Ten Courts maintain independent death records and do not simply inherit the Chancellor's commercial jurisdiction. Dock workers need liquidity and wages; depositors need accessible funds; small lenders fear selective forgiveness; administrators can support reform and still fear a run. These differences motivate the hearing21 rather than decorate a crowd.

Jinling purchases the bounty claim to suspend enforcement, not title to the party. The terms include departure and control of private information. Nil's mistaken comparison16 freezes a cooperative's reserve; Jinling cannot erase that cost by offering tea. Withdrawal17 must correct the actual claim and restore access, with witnesses retaining their right to criticize both guests and owner.

True accounts are preserved before enforcement copies burn. Relief below eight fen draws from distributable owner surplus, excluding deposits and wages. A provisional oversight board includes workers, depositors and municipal clerks; Jinling remains a disputed stakeholder. It grants investigation access21 and later returns a limited mandate42, rather than electing the travelling financier sovereign.

Bai Si and Cui record Wen through archival testimony and material work. A living descendant's account is not falsely described as eyewitness knowledge of an old death. Original data, interpretation and uncertainty are marked separately. Little Lantern's work and potential household relationship must include the child's wishes and stable care; an adopted child is not collateral acquired with a bounty purchase.

## Śūnyapura: knowledge has owners, conditions and wages

Scholar/guild councils control instrument schedules, water maintenance and publication access. Instrument crews need paid hours and safe monsoon access; scholars need independent observations; divers need enforceable return plans; surveyors can exploit permits. Research is material work as well as curiosity. A student can oppose censorship while supporting restrictions on personal data.

Shunya's failed model uses inert nonpersonal calibration pulses before permission to compare Meera's private record. Retrieval consent, comparison consent, a changed examination question and publication are different permissions. Nil's refusal26 does not terminate food, shelter or instruction. The later voluntary inert Place Value lesson is not a concealed repeat personal examination.

Jinling fears that data release can expose reserve clients; Shunya fears that restricted access can conceal a public harm. They agree to publish aggregate methods, failed tests and signed technical claims while protecting identifiable account records unless permission is separately granted. Neither must become morally stupid to sustain the disagreement. Vidya retains the failed appendix and crew wages as institutional obligations.

Seal0 and opened sky27 allow independent measurements; they do not hand the observatory unrestricted control of Nil. The20th-day dispatch requests a bounded transition, carries limits and names the still-unproven scale. The council's later mandate conditions participation on maintaining water and weather protection. An epilogue observation can be incomplete without implying science has failed.

## Kroton: duty, complicity and work after defection

The Synod maintains real shelters and routes while attaching access to a false ranking of numerical worth. Some residents support it because shelter works for them. The undercity supplies repair labour excluded from official credit. Kallisto organizes residents but cannot presume every resident accepts a single political remedy. Witnesses record disagreements about the pace of opening terraces.

Prima's previous denial of a family is a specific administrative act, not merely a feeling of doubt. The school rescue31 breaks an order, costs her office protection and requires residents' expertise. Kallisto's distrust is justified even when Prima saves children. Prima surrenders access logs, accepts supervision and later testifies. The Archon's life and his authority are separate; rescuing him35 does not reinstall him.

The Moirai define different trial functions: comprehensible sequence, permitted relationship measurement and an ending with a disclosed expendable alternative. No trial compels a companion sacrifice or consumes a protected proof. Their record is not consent to a future political use. The small rescue-team test works in a bounded channel but cannot scale until monopoly locks are released.

The35 reopening hearing creates a resident route committee and preserves inspection logs. Essential shelter crews remain employed; exclusions can be appealed independently. The mandate43 is provisional, not a fantasy of unanimous immediate democracy. Longer-term representative selection and permanent rules belong to the year of work shown in49, not a constitution invented during one fight.

## Crossroads: property, family and consent under unequal power

The House controls casino property, wages, credit and a commissioned protection instrument. The sheriff has public authority but depends on House financing. Employees need wages after the owner's departure; debtors need protection from retaliation; Fibi needs a right to leave not contingent on gratitude. The local loop intensifies these existing relationships rather than substituting for them.

TR999 commissions protection when Fibi is15; activation occurs inTR1002 when she is18. The party enters iteration1,597 on the same outside night defined inPhase2. A cancellation requires both the House's relinquishment of its control claim and Fibi's separate informed choice. Staff and creditors retain claims that cannot be settled by a grandfather's tender goodbye.

Penny's tokens and Vance's case markers are authorized retained records, not farmable fen. Penny's release follows discharge of the trapping obligation under the witnessed cancellation. An additional token may record the completed release; it is not a newly created spendable coin that purchases freedom. Fibi is not killed again to prove the reset mechanism. Her childhood protection is not retroactive adult consent.

Vesper's testimony and restitution are directed through Mara's chosen office. Mara may refuse a meeting and later change her mind without either action becoming a reward owed to Vesper. Fibi may end a conversation about Vesper's guilt. Vance supplies independent corroboration and discloses her office's financing. The41 staff office preserves wage and property claims after the clause closes.

## Basilica return and bounded transition

Regional replies42 and ratification43 convey explicit mandates from specific bodies, with dissent recorded. They authorize a trial transition and access to named emergency pools, not permanent submission to the companions. Resident and technical stewards can withdraw their own contribution before commitment; the final command must state when reversal would itself endanger coverage. Nil does not become a substitute Comptroller.

The rejected Board proposal, Ivo's copies, the failed timing test, local rescue result and preserved originals are independent evidence. Thale must confront these before choosing coercion46–47. Defeat48 removes executive control while witness custody preserves accountability. The final application49 changes the normalization regime; it does not promise infinite stock, resurrection or a world without storms.

An interim joint panel publishes source/recipient counter-records and hears appeals. Essential transport, treatment and water pools continue under regional stewards. Emergency powers expire after the bounded transition unless renewed through recorded local review; permanent forced Civic settlement is prohibited. The year-later scenes establish resident-selected representation and continuing disagreements. Thale faces proceedings with evidence and a defence; the book does not predeclare an unwritten verdict or pardon through an image of pigeons.

## Continuing roles after recruitment

The following responsibilities prevent companions becoming silent proof carriers between their home region and the ending. They supplement the Phase2 scene units; they do not add chapters, grant early powers or require every traveller in every panel.

**Volume1:** Nil chooses intervention over a route to sponsorship. Vesper demonstrates useful coordination while controlling what she discloses. Maeve negotiates departure with work obligations rather than appearing as a free closing gift. Ama and Livia remain people with work and wishes beyond the person protecting them. Other core companions have not joined and are not inserted through a premature cameo.

**Volume2:** Hina's household conflict and feared future lead the volume. Maeve's repair error gives their disagreement material stakes. Vesper must let a local childlike spirit lead a route she cannot master by bluffing. Nil's refusal to decide Hina's answer contrasts with their own search for a correct answer. The absent River and observatory cast are not omniscient advisers.

**Volume3:** Jinling's shelter terms and Bai Si's preservation of Wen's testimony expose different kinds of authority. Nil's false certainty has consequences before it earns a correction. Vesper's recognized mark becomes a disclosure she would rather postpone. Maeve helps copy the jar fittings and identifies what physical records cannot show; Hina insists Enma's counter-copy is evidence with a custodian, not a deed to Nil. Neither invents a solution to local banking or death records.

**Volume4:** Shunya's ambition, failed model and negotiated access carry the inquiry. Jinling challenges publication that would expose clients; Bai Si checks what belongs to a person and what belongs to a count. Hina recognizes the pressure to prove one is real but does not dictate Nil's response. Maeve maintains instruments under crew supervision rather than intuiting astronomy. Vesper offers an exit before a test and accepts Nil choosing differently. Nil both refuses and later chooses instruction; neither action has to be defended as bravery.

**Volume5:** Prima and Kallisto confront compliance and resident authority. Maeve's craft and Bai Si's witness method become necessary to preserve an old gear's history. Shunya declares the corrected rescue model's limits; Jinling names the team's stake and the pool that cannot be borrowed from. Hina helps communicate a route to frightened children without claiming magic makes them safe. Vesper follows residents' evacuation priorities before asking the party to risk her former employer's territory.

**Volume6:** Fibi controls what the group may learn about her own repetition. Vesper's task is accountability without demanding emotional payment. Jinling traces the maintenance debit rather than simply buying the beneficiary. Shunya distinguishes local iterations from outside dates; Bai Si records one person's repeated experiences rather than pretending1596 deaths are1596 different people. Maeve inspects physical interfaces but cannot repair consent by replacing a gear. Prima accepts Fibi's instruction to step back. Hina asks which ordinary future Fibi wants rather than lending her a prewritten wish list.

**Volume7:** Nil chooses a bounded attempt and declines hereditary power. Hina, Jinling, Bai Si, Shunya, Prima, Maeve, Vesper and Fibi retain individual authority over their records and a right to ask what the proposed operation means. Their cooperation is enacted through the distinct practices already tested, not merged into a moral score. The epilogue gives each a separate obligation: Hina's care/festival, Jinling's contested oversight, Bai Si's disputed count, Shunya's restricted publication, Prima's testimony/teaching, Maeve's request-led craft, Vesper's restitution and Fibi's chosen road. None requires permanent membership in Nil's travelling group.

## Voice and acting tests

These are audition lines, not dialogue automatically inserted into a chapter. Nil: “I said it matched. I hadn't checked the date.” Vesper: “Put my name on the statement. Leave hers off the story you tell about me.” Maeve: “The rib holds. The patch doesn't belong to me.” Hina: “Hina wanted a new roof. Not a new sky.” Jinling: “If I spend their wages to forgive their debts, I've charged them twice.” Bai Si: “Keep both accounts. Write down why they differ.” Shunya: “That part is measured. This part is still my guess.” Prima: “I signed that refusal. I will testify to it.” Fibi: “I know what you usually say. Let me finish this time.”

Differentiate syntax and the purpose of humour. Nil's literal precision becomes awkward when feelings need no answer. Vesper deflects until testimony requires plainness. Maeve thinks through material operations; Hina expands ceremonially until genuine fear strips the flourish away. Jinling negotiates conditions; Bai Si names people; Shunya separates hypothesis from observation; Prima sheds institutional passive voice; Fibi loses fluent prediction when the future is truly new. No character is required to make a joke during someone else's suffering.

Before accepting a scene, verify a character wants something local, has knowledge obtained by that point, uses an instrument/skill they actually possess and can refuse an offered relationship. Check hands, eye line, held objects, spatial exits, social status and aftermath. A statement about consent must be followed by the practical ability to refuse. Narration must not turn a false doctrine into objective truth merely because an articulate person says it.

## Game-only and background scope

Kyūsuke, Granny Liu Shun and Baital retain their existing game/visual identities but have no required named scene in the current49-chapter central novel. They are optional adaptation candidates, not missing ending prerequisites. Introducing one later requires a ledger change that gives the appearance a purpose without adding an obligatory companion or proof. Reusable encounter archetypes and faction uniforms in the91-record art catalog are visual/game content, not a claim that every figure must recur in the novel.

Background labels such as workers, children and guards represent people with different interests. They are not singular minds. The structured cast-coverage map distinguishes named profiles, machines, groups and indirect appearances. Chapter21's Ivo-by-record and chapter42's Livia-by-letter do not teleport those characters into the room. The final all-eight-core label expands to Vesper, Maeve, Hina, Jinling, Bai Si, Shunya, Prima and Fibi; Nil is additional.
