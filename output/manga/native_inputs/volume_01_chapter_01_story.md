# Chapter 1: Three Bowls

A young unregistered wanderer uses NIL privately, copied from their wrist decoder; they have not chosen a personal name. They want a repair apprenticeship because it includes a bed. The employer needs an account; the registrar demands a sponsor. The ninth bell closes the opportunity while a delivery cylinder is processed instantly.

Rain drives them beneath Baker's awning. A muddy bun costs one fen, but their polished washer is no money. Baker suggests Gus at the Bazaar and offers a bun for washing three bowls. Tobi jokes that they should wash already-clean ones. His mother Ama arrives with a repaired coat, saved thread and Civic01. She accepts the answer that the wanderer has not decided what to be called.

At the market arch a Collector retains Ama's corrected receipt and begins an enforcement action beside a jar holding six lights. The wanderer puts their gloves back on. The Bazaar road and its possible sponsor remain available. They choose to step between Tobi and the case: Before you take anything else, whose six are those?
