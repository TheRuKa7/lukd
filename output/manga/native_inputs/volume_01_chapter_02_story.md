# Chapter 2: The Jar Owes Six

Ama paid the fee Tuesday; the Collector's notice retains an obsolete authorization. He keeps her receipt while telling her to request another. Her last six Civic units were confiscated earlier and their return is still pending. He is about to take her remaining one. At Civic00, ferry travel needs an escort and the clinic needs a guarantee.

The wanderer's physical attempt to pull the lever fails, scraping the RIGHT palm through a wet glove. They see an escape stair but stay. The jar, Ama's receipt and an empty return fitting form a visible route. Their RIGHT decoder bridges it; the LEFT hand reverses the lever. Six existing lights leave the jar and arrive in Ama's account:01 becomes07, the jar empties, NaN remains unchanged. Uncertain impressions of helping hands are not an origin explanation.

Ama retrieves her receipt and embraces Tobi. Baker gives the bun to Tobi, who passes it into the wanderer's LEFT hand. The Collector sees NaN and whistles. A Debt Hound enters on four stamp-shaped mechanical feet. Baker directs the family to the laundry stairs. The wanderer draws the Hound toward the canal service route instead.
