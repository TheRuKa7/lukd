# Chapter 3: A Door That Opens

The Debt Hound blocks the service stair. Its lunge catches a railing; the wanderer ducks past and takes seven steps beneath the bridge, moving away from the family. Their RIGHT palm hurts. They pocket the bun from the LEFT hand. A peg catches their longer LEFT coat tail; the Hound blunders through a sheet and briefly resembles an angry parcel.

A courtyard gate locks. The decoder cannot open an unrelated mechanism without the jar's return connection. A resident helps turn her laundry cart across the lane, risking her washing. The wanderer reaches a door carved with an open hand. Nought hides his lamp and advises the quiet latch instead of a squeaking bolt.

He offers a repaired chair, clean water and cloth before questions. He asks before dressing the RIGHT palm. The crushed bun is shared, with the larger piece returned. The wanderer sleeps until late afternoon. They explain Ama's six-unit return and admit wanting an account and a name. Nought says they are already a person. Rest, a clear back exit and a Naming attempt remain separate choices. He can explain choices, not choose for them.
